The task

Essay

A famous songwriter once said, ‘Expose yourself to your deepest fear; after that, fear has no power.’

How important is it for us to acknowledge the challenges we face and find ways to cope with them? Write an essay explaining your opinion with relevant examples from your personal experience.

  • State your opinion clearly in the introduction.
  • Support your view with personal examples. Link back to the quote where you can.

Draft length (student), about 480 words.

Useful words acknowledge(承認), cope with(應對), challenge(挑戰), overcome(克服), confidence(自信), embrace(接納), opportunity(機會), grow(成長)

Marking (CLO)

HKDSE Paper 2 is assessed on Content, Language, and Organisation. The levels below are indicative only for study, not official HKEAA marks.

Content, Level 4

  • Clear opinion that acknowledging challenges and coping with them is very important.
  • Three personal examples (presentation, card club, maths exam) fit the task.
  • You return to the songwriter quote in the conclusion.
  • To push higher, make each example feel less like the same pattern (fear → action → success).

Language, Level 3–4

  • Ideas are easy to follow, but watch verb forms (grow/growing, will/would in past stories).
  • Fix double comparatives (more stronger) and a few broken phrases in the conclusion.
  • Use task words like cope with naturally, not only deal with.

Organisation, Level 4–5

  • Strong essay shape. Intro → First → Also → Furthermore → conclusion.
  • Each body paragraph has a topic idea plus one example.
  • Conclusion sums up your three benefits (stronger, confident, wiser).

How to do better next time

  • Vary your examples. You do not need three stories that all end with “I succeeded.” One could show a small step forward, not full success.
  • Past-tense stories. In a personal example, use I would forget, not I will forget, when you look back on the past.
  • One idea per sentence in the ending. The last body of the conclusion is doing too much in one line. Split it into two short sentences.

Overall (indicative). About Level 4 for content and organisation. Language accuracy is the main limit on the band.

Student draft

A famous songwriter said that, “Expose yourself to your deepest fear; after that, fear has no power.” I believe that this idea is true. It is important for us to acknowledge the challenges we face and find solutions to deal with them. When we face the fears, we become stronger and more confident. Ignoring problems or running away from them will only make us feel guilty and less brave. Facing challenges helps us growing stronger and learning how to deal with difficulties in real life.

First, when we acknowledge our fears, we need to first understand what the main reason bother us. Sometimes, we are afraid of getting a fail, losing a job, or communicating with new people. If we ignore these fears, they can become bigger and control us. But if we accept and embrace that we are afraid and try to understand the reason behind it, we can find ways to solve the problem. For example, I was very nervous before giving a presentation in front of the whole class. I was afraid I will forget the script and make mistakes. But I told myself to face my fear and keep practicing my speech. When the day came, I was still nervous and sweating, but I was more prepared. In the end, I did my very best and presented successfully. This experience taught me that facing my fears made me feel more stronger.

Also, facing our fears can help us build confidence. When we try to overcome challenges, we realise that we are stronger than we think. For example, I used to be shy and afraid to make new friends. But in one day, I decided to join a card game club at school and communicate with new classmates. At first, I couldn't even move a step, but I ordered myself to speak and participate in playing cards. Slowly, I made new friends and felt happier as I got teammates to fight with me. This showed me that facing my fears helped me become more confident and happier in life.

Furthermore, dealing with challenges teaches us important lessons that cannot be learnt in classroom. There are problems everytime , but each time we face them, we learn new knowledge or experience. We learn to be brave, patient, and strong. For example, when I failed my math exam, I felt upset and disappointed. But I decided to study harder in what I weak in and ask for help from my teacher. The next test, I did much better. This taught me that those challenges are opportunities to learn and let me grow.

In conclusion, it is very important for us to acknowledge our challenges and find ways to cope with them. When we face our fears, we become stronger, more confident, and wiser. Living in this world will always full with problems, but after we deal with them will make us to be a better person. Just like the songwriter said, exposing ourselves to our deepest fears can take away their power. I believe that facing our fears is the best way to help us grow and succeed in life.
