The task

Proposal

You are the Chairperson of the Community Heritage Club. Many traditional family-run shops in your area are closing.

Write a proposal to the District Council. Explain why these shops are important. Suggest two practical ways to encourage more young people to visit and support these local businesses.

  • Open with who you are and the problem.
  • Give reasons before your two suggestions. Keep each suggestion focused on young people where you can.

Draft length (student), about 490 words.

Useful words family-run(家庭式), heritage(文化傳承), livelihood(生計), rent pressure(租金壓力), exposure(曝光), social media(社交媒體), financial support(財政支援), local business(本地小店), tradition(傳統)

Marking (CLO)

HKDSE Paper 2 Part B is assessed on Content, Language, and Organisation. The levels below are indicative only for study, not official HKEAA marks.

Content, Level 4

  • Clear problem and a strong local example (Happy Baking).
  • Three reasons why shops matter. history, tourism, livelihood.
  • Two suggestions given. Social media fits young people well.
  • Second suggestion is mostly government funding. Link it more clearly to youth visits or add a youth-focused plan.

Language, Level 3

  • Many ideas, but grammar and word choice slip often.
  • Watch fewer, verb agreement, articles and prepositions.
  • Some sentences repeat the same idea. Tighter writing would raise the band.

Organisation, Level 4

  • Problem → importance → suggestions → closing is easy to follow.
  • First and Furthermore signpost the two suggestions.
  • The importance section is long. One reason per short paragraph would read more like a proposal.

How to do better next time

  • Youth focus. For each suggestion, say how it gets young people into the shops, e.g. IG reels, school heritage walks, student discounts.
  • Trim repetition. History, tourism and jobs are all valid, but you repeat them in long sentences. One clear point per paragraph is enough.
  • Proposal tone. Start suggestions with We propose that… or Our club suggests… so it sounds written to the District Council.

Overall (indicative). About Level 4 for content and organisation. Language accuracy and sharper youth-focused suggestions are the main limits.

Student draft

Proposal for Supporting Local Businesses

Our Community Heritage Club has found out that many traditional family-run shops in our area are closing rapidly in recent years. For example, a bakery shop, called “Happy Baking”, opened since 1980, were closed in last year. The shop now becomes a claw-machine shop which made the area less attractive and dying. In fact, a lot of local small businesses were closed in these years as the high rent pressure and less people visiting. These traditional family-run shops with long history may now suffer in difficulties, deciding whether closing the shop to decrease the loss or keeping what they promise to their grandparents. However, they can do nothing for it.

These shops are important to Hong Kong as Hong Kong has a rich history. These shops are representing the history of Hong Kong. If we lose them, the memory of the old generation will be gone and the new generation will not know anything about the Hong Kong tradition and history. Also, these shops have their potential to save the bad economy of Hong Kong by using the tradition and the special style to attract tourist to visit Hong Kong. A glimpse of those traditional shops will cost tourists to stay at Hong Kong at least one night, which can help bring a better economy. And lastly, the most important thing is that the shop is the only way for them to earn a living. The owners used half of their lifetimes to learn the skills from their grandparents, which means that they may not have a degree from university or even secondary school. They may have difficulties in earning a living after the shop closed. When the shop closed this caused not only disappearing of the family memories, but also a decrease of money gain.

The local businesses are really important to all of us and we need to support them. Here are two ways that our club believes that can ease the problem.

First, creating a social media account for help those local businesses gain more exposure, letting the world see the special of the Hong Kong traditional family-run shops. Uploading reels in IG and videos in Youtube, sharing about the beauty of those shops can surely encourage not only young people in Hong Kong, but also all people around the world to visit. Also, invite KOLs and celebrities to film and introduce those local businesses shops can attract people and fans to come and try. Using social media to do marketing and personal introduction is needed and can be useful.

Furthermore, the government should give financial support to the local traditional shops. After the COVID-19 pandemic, there are lots of shops closed or almost closed as most of the people did not go out and closed to shop online. This scene caused lots of shops dying, because of gaining less profit. Giving them short-term financial support can reduce their pressure. This can also give time and resources for them to decide any further action to make themselves more competitive and spread out the special and the beauty of their skills and product. Money is always the most useful resources to solve problems.

We hope you can consider our suggestions and understand our concern about the dying of traditional local businesses. The situation now is getting intense and we need to focus on it now.

Comments

You answer the task with strong reasons and two clear suggestions. The yellow bits help you tighten grammar and keep each suggestion focused on young people.

ENStrong opening. The Happy Baking example makes the problem feel real. A claw-machine shop replacing an old bakery is easy to picture.

粵開首有力。 Happy Baking 例子令問題好具體。舊餅店變 claw-machine 鋪，讀者易想像。

ENClear reasons. You explain history, tourism and owners’ livelihoods. These are good reasons why the shops matter.

粵理由清楚。 你寫歷史、旅遊同店主生計，都係小店重要嘅原因。

ENSocial media idea. IG reels, YouTube and KOLs are practical ways to reach young people and bring them to local shops.

粵社交媒體建議。 IG reels、YouTube 同 KOL 都係接觸年輕人、帶佢哋去小店嘅實際方法。

ENYouth focus. The question asks how to get young people to visit. Say that clearly in both suggestions, e.g. school heritage walks or student discounts.

粵針對年輕人。 題目問點樣吸引年輕人光顧。兩個建議都要寫清楚，例如 heritage walk 或學生優惠。

ENGrammar. Watch fewer people, opened in 1980, chose to shop online and encourage tourists to stay. Shorter sentences will also help.

粵文法。 留意 fewer people、opened in 1980、chose to shop online、encourage tourists to stay。句子短啲會更易讀。

ENTrim repetition. The importance section repeats similar ideas. Keep one main point in each paragraph so the proposal stays focused.

粵刪重複。 講重要性嗰段有啲重複。每段一個重點，提案會更集中。
