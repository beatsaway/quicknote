The task

Essay / article

Online Learning: The Future of Education or a Passing Fad?

In recent years, online learning has grown quickly. Some people believe it will shape the future of education. Others think it is only a short-lived trend. What is your view? Write an essay explaining your opinion with reasons and examples.

  • State your view clearly (future of education or passing fad).
  • Support your side with two or three developed reasons before you deal with the other side.
  • Include a short rebuttal if the question asks you to consider both views.

Draft length (student), about 380 words.

Useful words online learning(網上學習), flexible(靈活), interactive(互動), face to face(面對面), assess(評估), misinformation(錯誤資訊), passing fad(短期熱潮，唔長久), trend(趨勢), effective(有效), classroom learning(課堂學習)

Structure first

Your teacher’s main point. Put your own reasons in the body first. Save the benefits of online learning for a rebuttal paragraph near the end.

Your draft order

  1. Intro + question
  2. Others’ benefits (dense, many points)
  3. Social skills + unstable internet
  4. Assessment + myopia
  5. Conclusion

Stronger order

  1. Intro + your view (passing fad)
  2. Your reason 1 (e.g. social skills)
  3. Your reason 2 (e.g. hard to assess online)
  4. Your reason 3 (optional, e.g. screen time)
  5. Rebuttal then answer it (flexibility, etc.)
  6. Conclusion

What went well

Lots of ideas and a clear question in the opening.

  • 👍 Many points. Flexibility, social life, internet, assessment and screen time all appear.
  • 👍 Good hook. COVID-19 and Zoom/Google Meet set the scene well.
  • 👍 Assessment point. Teachers finding it hard to assess students online can be developed further.
  • 👍 Clear ending. You say online learning is a fast trend, not the real future.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Order. Do not open the body with a long benefits paragraph. That is the other side, not your stance.
  • 💡 One idea per paragraph. Do not put social skills and unstable internet together. Do not put assessment and myopia together.
  • 💡 Develop, don’t pad. The social paragraph feels stretched next to the long benefits block. Make each of your reasons equally strong.
  • 💡 Whole-piece feel. Like writing a song, the essay needs one clear shape from start to finish.

Marking (CLO)

Indicative levels from your teacher’s feedback. For study only, not official HKEAA marks.

Content, Level 4–5

  • Plenty of relevant ideas. flexibility, social life, assessment, screens.
  • Assessment online is a strong point to expand with an example.
  • Internet stability is weaker. develop or cut.

Language, Level 4

  • Ideas are clear. Reader can follow you.
  • Watch verb forms, articles and fixed phrases (at any time, whether).
  • Capitalise platform names (Zoom, Google Meet, YouTube).

Organisation, Level 3

  • Benefits paragraph comes too early and packs too many points.
  • Some paragraphs mix two ideas that do not belong together.
  • Reordering body paragraphs would raise this band quickly.

Next draft plan

  • Paragraph 2. State your view. Online learning is mainly a passing fad.
  • Paragraphs 3–4. Two strong reasons on your side. social skills and fair assessment.
  • Paragraph 5. Admittedly, supporters say… then answer the flexibility point in two sentences.
  • Conclusion. One short line. classroom learning still matters after COVID-19.

Overall (indicative). About Level 4. Content and language are solid. Organisation is the main thing holding the piece back.

Student draft

Online Learning: The Future of Education or a Passing Fad?

IntroIn recent years, online learning has become a very popular trend. Many schools and universities started using the internet such as zoom and google meet to teach students. COVID-19 made this trend happen fast as students had to stay at home for preventing infection and learn from their computers or iPads. However, COVID-19 stopped and people now wonder that is online learning the future of education, or is it just a fast trend?

Body 1 · move later (others’ benefits)online learning users say that it bring various of benefits. It is flexible which means that students can study at anytime, in anywhere. It is helpful for students who are busy, having family gatherings, or live far from schools. Online classes also offer many fun and interactive activities such as quizzes and videos. Students can replay recorded lessons and learn at their own speed. This can save travel time and let students study from home comfortably.

Body 2 · split into two paragraphsHowever, studying online may affect students’ social skills. Students have fewer chances to communicate face to face with classmates. They may feel lonely and find it hard to build friendships. Also, some students do not have stable internet connection at home. When the network is weak, they may miss important parts of the lesson.

Body 3 · split (assessment vs myopia)Moreover, teachers may find it difficult to assess students’ ability accurately during online lessons. Some students may cheat in online tests or copy answers from the Internet. In addition, staring at screens for a long time may cause myopia. Many students need to wear glasses because they use computers too much every day.

ConclusionIn conclusion, I think online learning is just a fast trend, not the real future of education. Although it helped during COVID-19, face-to-face classroom learning is still more effective for most students.

Comments

You already have plenty of ideas and a clear view at the end. The biggest upgrade is structure. Put your own reasons first, then rebut the benefits paragraph.

ENMany strong ideas. You cover flexibility, social life, the internet, assessment and screen time. That gives you a lot to work with in the next draft.

粵論點多。 靈活、社交、網絡、評估、用眼都有寫到，改結構之後會好有力。

ENGood opening. COVID-19, Zoom and the question at the end of paragraph 1 fit the topic well.

粵開首好。 疫情、Zoom 同段尾問句都貼題。

ENAssessment point. Teachers finding it hard to assess students online is worth developing. Add one short example, e.g. cheating in a test.

粵評估呢點有用。 老師難以網上評估學生值得寫深，加一個短例子，例如網上測驗作弊。

ENBenefits paragraph too early. That block is dense and it is not your stance. Write two or three paragraphs supporting passing fad first, then add one rebuttal paragraph for the benefits.

粵好處段唔應放最前。 嗰段好密，又唔係你立場。你先寫兩三段撐自己嘅睇法，最後先駁返網上學習有咩好處。

ENOne idea per paragraph. Social skills and unstable internet are two different points. Assessment and myopia should not share one paragraph either.

粵一段一個重點。 社交同網絡唔穩定係兩點，唔好撈埋。評估同近視都唔應同一段。

ENWhole-piece shape. Like writing a song, the essay needs one clear flow. Right now the long benefits block makes readers think the other side is stronger before they reach your view.

粵全篇要有整體感。 好似作歌咁，成篇要順。而家好處段太長，讀者會以為對方好勁，未到你立場已先入為主。

ENDevelop, don’t pad. The social paragraph feels like word-count padding next to the benefits section. Give each of your own reasons the same care as the flexibility examples.

粵要寫深唔好撐字數。 社交嗰段比起好處段似拉長湊字。你自己立場嘅每點都要寫到同樣扎實。
