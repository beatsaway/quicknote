The task

School magazine article

Some believe that studying abroad is always better for a student’s future. Others argue that staying in Hong Kong for university is equally beneficial.

Write an article discussing this issue. Present arguments for and against studying abroad. Offer your own perspective and reasoning. Body paragraphs stay on one side (agree or disagree).

  • Curtis chose the disagree side. Staying in Hong Kong is equally beneficial.
  • Aim for at least three developed points with Hong Kong examples.

Draft length (student), about 265 words.

Useful words ranking(排名), subsidised(資助), living costs(生活費), familiar environment(熟悉環境), UGC-funded(教資會資助), internship(實習), alumni(校友), perspective(觀點)

What went well

You stay on one side and cover three clear themes.

  • 👍 Three main points. University quality, language, and cost each have their own paragraph.
  • 👍 HK examples. Top-100 rankings, UGC fees, UK/USA living costs feel relevant.
  • 👍 Clear view. Your conclusion says Hong Kong is a strong choice instead of studying abroad.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Magazine opening. Start with a short hook to the reader, not only a general statement about rankings.
  • 💡 One example per point. Name one local university, one language situation, or one cost comparison to make each paragraph sharper.
  • 💡 Grammar. Watch subject–verb agreement, word choice (medicine not medication) and prepositions (with the culture).

Comments

You already have three strong reasons and a clear conclusion. The yellow bits are mostly grammar and word choice, not a rewrite of your ideas.

ENOne side only. You argue that staying in Hong Kong is equally beneficial. Your body stays on that side, which matches the homework.

粵只寫一邊。 你論證留港升學同樣好，正文跟足呢個方向，符合功課要求。

ENThree points. Quality, language and cost are easy to follow. Each paragraph has a clear main idea.

粵三個重點。 質素、語言、費用三段分明，每段有主題。

ENGood contrast. In paragraph 2 you compare HK with studying abroad. That makes your disagree side stronger.

粵對比好。 第二段同出國讀書比較，支持「留港同樣好」更有力。

ENSharper examples. Try one concrete detail per point, e.g. HKU/CUHK ranking, using Cantonese in daily life, or UGC tuition vs UK rent.

粵例子再具體。 每段加一個細節，例如港大排名、日常用廣東話、教資會學費同英国租金比較。

ENGrammar patterns. Watch rankings are, living costs are, studying abroad, and familiarise with. These appear several times in your draft.

粵文法位。 留意 rankings are、living costs are、studying abroad、familiarise with，稿裏出現多次。

ENMagazine tone. Open with a question or scene for school readers, e.g. “Should we all leave Hong Kong for university?” Then give your view.

粵校刊語氣。 開首可用問句或情景吸引同學，再講你立場。

Demo writing

Same task and the same side as your draft. Compare structure, examples and links with your own. This is one possible Level 5 version, not the only correct answer.

Staying in Hong Kong for University Is Equally Beneficial

Many students assume that a successful future depends on studying abroad. In my view, that is not always true. Hong Kong universities can offer an education that is just as valuable.

To commence with, local universities rank among the best in the world. Institutions such as HKU and CUHK regularly appear in global top-100 lists. Many courses are taught by experienced professors, some of whom trained overseas, and students can use modern laboratories and equipment. Graduates often build successful careers in fields such as medicine, law and finance. In other words, the standard of teaching here can match what students might find abroad.

Another crucial point is that students can study in a familiar language environment. In class, they may follow lectures in English and discuss ideas with classmates in Cantonese or Putonghua. They do not need months to adjust before they can concentrate on their degree. By contrast, studying overseas often means using one main language all day, rebuilding friendships and learning how daily life works in a new place before their studies truly get going.

Last but not least, the cost of studying locally is usually lower. UGC-funded places keep tuition fees more affordable for many families. Living at home also saves rent and daily expenses. In the UK or the US, tuition, rent, food and transport can place heavy pressure on ordinary families.

To conclude, Hong Kong now offers a mature and well-developed university system. Students who want a strong education do not have to go abroad to find it. Staying here can save time and money while still providing knowledge and opportunities that are comparable with universities overseas. For many of us, that makes Hong Kong the smarter choice.
