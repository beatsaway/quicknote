The task

Nomination statement

You are running for the position of House Captain or Student Union Chairperson. Write a nomination statement to be posted on the school notice board.

Write about 200 words. Include a heading and use these two subheadings. Past Contributions and Experience. Commitment to the Student Body.

  • Say who you are and which post you want.
  • Under each subheading, give clear examples, not only a list of roles.

Draft length (student), about 265 words.

Useful words nominate(提名), house captain(社長), contribution(貢獻), experience(經驗), commitment(承諾), lead(帶領), team spirit(團隊精神), morale(士氣), represent(代表)

Marking (CLO)

HKDSE Paper 2 Part A is assessed on Content, Language, and Organisation. The levels below are indicative only for study, not official HKEAA marks.

Content, Level 4

  • Clear candidate (Chris Wong, F.4A, Love House Captain) and both required subheadings.
  • Strong past experience (helper, captain, cheering leader, house sports).
  • The basketball story is vivid and personal. Good for persuading classmates.
  • Commitment section is thinner. Say what you will do next year, not only that you are fit.

Language, Level 3

  • Ideas are clear, but watch past tenses (I have been vs I had been).
  • Fix word choice (calm, in the same boat, well suited to this role).
  • Last sentence stops mid-idea (spirit of Love House and trust).

Organisation, Level 4–5

  • Heading, intro, two subheadings and a short close match the task shape well.
  • Experience paragraph mixes roles and one long example. That still works, but keep the close sharper.

How to do better next time

  • Commitment. Give two concrete plans, e.g. more house activities, listening to junior students, building team spirit after matches.
  • Trim the intro. One sentence to say who you are and what post you want is enough.
  • One strong story. The basketball moment is your best line. You do not need as many general phrases about glory and trust.

Overall (indicative). About Level 4 for content and organisation. Language accuracy and a fuller commitment section are the main limits.

Student draft

Nomination of Love House Captain

I am Chris Wong from F.4A. I am interested in being the Love House Captain, so I am nominating myself for the position of House Captain. Here are my contributions and experience in the past and what I will commit to the student body:

Past Contributions and Experience

I had been a Love House helper for two years and a Love House captain for one year. In these years, I had joined different house activities like house basketball competition and house volleyball competition. We had won a champion and get some good grades, which made me feel that me and Love House are in the same ship, getting through difficulties together. Also, I had been a cheering leader of Love House, which increased the confidence of Love House people.

The most unforgettable experience is that in last year, the House Captain comforted me to stay clam in a basketball competition. He passed the ball to me and shouted out “You can do it Chris!” when there was only 3 second left and the marks of Love House and Kwan House were 80:81. I was wide open and successfully shot the ball into the basket, taking the win.

Commitment to the Student Body

I am sure that I am totally fit on this position of Love House Captain and can keep the glory of Love House shine. I will try my best to lead everyone in Love House and let the spirit of Love House and trust.

Comments

You follow the task shape and your basketball story is the strongest part. The yellow bits are mostly grammar and a stronger commitment ending, not a rewrite of your ideas.

ENTask shape. You include a heading and both subheadings. A marker can see you followed the notice-board format.

粵符合格式。 有標題同兩個小標，跟足提名聲明格式。

ENStrong story. The last-second basket with the score 80–81 is vivid. That kind of detail helps classmates remember you.

粵故事有力。 80 比 81 臨終前入波好生动，同學會記得你。

ENReal experience. Helper, captain, cheering leader and house sports show you already serve Love House, not only talk about it.

粵有真實經歷。 做過 helper、captain、cheering leader 同社際比賽，唔係空口講。

ENCommitment section. Say what you will do if elected, e.g. run more house events, support junior students, keep morale high after losses.

粵承諾部分。 寫當選後會做咩，例如多搞社活動、幫师弟妹、輸波後都保持士氣。

ENGrammar. Watch I have been, stay calm, three seconds and in the same boat. Finish the last sentence clearly.

粵文法。 留意 I have been、stay calm、three seconds、in the same boat。最後一句要寫完整。

ENShorter intro. Cut repeated lines about nominating yourself. One clear opening sentence is enough before the subheadings.

粵開首精簡。 提名自己唔使重複講兩次。小標前一句清楚自我介紹就夠。
