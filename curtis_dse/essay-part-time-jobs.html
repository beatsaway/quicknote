Part-time jobs for secondary students

Many Form 6 students discuss whether they should take part-time jobs. Some believe working part-time develops important skills. Others argue that students should focus on their studies. What is your view? Write an essay explaining your opinion with reasons and examples.

  • State your view clearly in the introduction.
  • Give two strong reasons for your side before you deal with the opposite view.
  • Include a short rebuttal, then conclude.

Draft length (student), about 320 words.

Useful words part-time job(兼職), responsibility(責任感), time management(時間管理), workload(工作量), real-world experience(真實工作經驗), balance(平衡), prioritise(分優先次序), academic studies(學業), revision(溫習), sleep deprivation(睡眠不足)

Should secondary students take part-time jobs?

Many secondary students, especially Form 6 students, discussing whether they should take part-time jobs or not. Some believe that working part-time can help develop important skills, while others argue that students should focus on their education. Personally, I believe that part-time work can be beneficial for students if their time managed properly, but it must not affect with their academic priority.

First and foremost, engaging in part-time work allows students to develop life skills such as responsibility and time-management skills. These skills are valuable for their future careers which cannot be learnt in school. For example, handling a limited working time job in a restaurant teaches students how to manage workload effectively without becoming overwhelmed.

Second of all, part-time jobs can provide real-world experience that enhances students understanding of various working environments and atmosphere. This knowledge can be useful during oral and writing exam, as it broadens their horizons and builds confidence of communication. Furthermore, working as a part-time can also teach students about the balance between work and study, helping them to set a timetable and prioritise their tasks.

However, that is not entirely the case, as opponents argue that part-time job might minimise their attention span and reduce study hours, leading to lack of revision time and inconsistent performance. They believe students should focus on their academic, especially during test and exam periods. While this concern is true, it can be prevented by setting limited working hours and adding workload management. For example, students can choose age-appropriate jobs that do not distract their revision schedule or sleeping time, which can avoid sleep deprivation and stress.

To conclude, while there must be risks involved, part-time work is a good idea for students if they maintain a work and study life balance and manage their time wisely. When these measures are done, it can help them develop valuable skills and get prepared for the future challenges.
