The task

Diary entry

Your school, Munsang College, is celebrating its 100th anniversary. You volunteered at the Centenary Open Day.

Write a diary entry about the day. Describe what you did, how you felt, and what the experience meant to you as part of the “Munsang Family” (about 350–450 words).

  • Register should sound personal and reflective, like a real diary.
  • Include at least one vivid moment (e.g. in the laboratory) and a closing thought.

Draft length (student), about 420 words.

Useful words centenary(百周年), volunteer(義工), atmosphere(氣氛), grateful(感恩), instructor(講解員), souvenir(紀念品), impression(印象), reflect(反思), school spirit(校園精神)

Marking (CLO)

HKDSE Paper 2 is assessed on Content, Language, and Organisation. The levels below are indicative only for study, not official HKEAA marks.

Content, Level 4

  • Clear diary voice and a full day (arrival, lab duty, visitors, souvenir, “Munsang Family”).
  • Strong personal detail (chemistry lab, mole question, periodic-table gift, PE memo pack).
  • Reflection on growth and school identity fits the task well.
  • To push higher, trim repetition in the lab paragraph and sharpen one opening hook.

Language, Level 3

  • Ideas are clear, but tense, articles, and word form slip often (came/come, an instructor, information).
  • Watch spelling (Munsang, prize, about) and a few broken sentences.
  • Collocation needs polish (grateful for, impression on me, memories for me).

Organisation, Level 4

  • Logical flow from morning purpose → lab work → reflection → souvenir → closing meaning.
  • Paragraph breaks match stages of the day.
  • Final paragraph on “Munsang Family” gives a strong sense of ending.

How to do better next time

  • Diary openers. After Dear diary, use a complete sentence for the date or occasion (Today is our centenary Open Day).
  • Past tense. Once the day has started, keep events in the past (came, brought, felt).
  • One scene at a time. Show one visitor moment in detail, then summarise the rest, instead of repeating the same action for five hours.
  • Proofread names. Keep Munsang spelled the same throughout.

Overall (indicative). About Level 3–4. Strong content and school spirit; language accuracy holds the band.

Student draft

Dear diary,

Today in my school, Munsang College, 100th Anniversary. It is an important day for Munsang College as the road to 100 years is not easy. I feel so grateful about studying in this school which brings me joy and knowledge in these 14 years. As a part of the ‘Munsang Family’, I decided to join the Centenary Open Days and be a volunteer in it.

When I entered the gate of the school, I could feel the greatness of this school. The atmosphere on campus was joyful and the laugh from the people spread to every corner of the school. I rushed to the meeting room and found the teachers, finding what I can do in today. My Chemistry teacher come to find me and gave me a task which was being a instructor in the laboratory. I read the informations of the equipment of the laboratory and tried to remember all the knowledge that I learnt from the chemistry class.

After a few minutes, the first laboratory visitor come in I brought him to look around the equipment and share what students will do in here. Then, I asked him some questions whaut basic chemistry. The visitor got them all right. He even did the bonus question which was about calculation of mole, I clapped for him and gave one of the biggest library souvenir, which is a periodic table with MSC logo. I'm sure that people who love chemistry will love this price. I kept repeating the same thing in about 5 hours. There were about 90 visitors come in and all of them left the room with a smile, I felt success in this work as I could introduce the functions of the equipment fluently and I could reply their questions vering what I have learnt in chemistry lessons. Furthermore, I experienced communicating with people outside the campus.

This opportunity makes me feel that I am growing up as I can talk with strangers and finish my task , I won't forget what I did today.

Speaking of souvenir, I saw an interesting which is a memo pack with MSC PE uniform design. It made a deep impression into my brain. Our school PE uniform is special for the colour, Yellow and red , which usually been called as tomato with eggs. I think it is funny to have a tomato and egg colored memo sticking at home or workplace , reminding me to do what . Seriously, the main reason why I love it is because my favourite lesson in school is PE lesson. This lesson creates a lot of sad and happy memories to me, having this memo pack can keep me rewind all of the good days in MSC after I graduate.

Sometimes I think about the meaning of ‘Munsung Family’ , and Now I finally know it. It means that the students who study in here, the teachers teaching their subject in here the cleaning workers tidying the classroom and the head principals who keep maintaining the school well . Because of what we achieved , Munsang College can keep improving, keep walking to now. We should work together, Keeping our school to shine forever.

Comments

You capture real Open Day detail and genuine pride in Munsang. The yellow highlights mark grammar and wording upgrades. Use the CLO table for band sense.

ENDiary voice. Open with one clear sentence about the day (Today is our centenary Open Day), then your feelings. That sounds more natural than a list of facts.

粵日記語氣。 開首用一句清楚交代今日係百周年開放日，再寫感受，會比逐樣列事實更似真日記。

ENLab scene. One visitor answering the mole question is your best moment. Describe his reaction and your gift in one short paragraph, then say you repeated this for about five hours and ninety visitors.

粵實驗室場面。 訪客答啱 mole 計算題係全篇最亮點。先細寫佢嘅反應同你送嘅紀念品，再一句帶過之後五個鐘、大約九十位訪客，唔使重複同一套動作。

ENLocal colour. “Tomato and eggs” (番茄炒蛋) for the PE colours is vivid and very HK. Keep it, but fix the sentence around it so the joke lands cleanly.

粵本地味道。 用「番茄炒蛋」形容 PE 衫紅黃配色好有香港味，值得留。執好前後句子，個笑位先會順。

ENEnding. Your “Munsang Family” idea is strong. List students, teachers, cleaners, and leaders in separate short phrases, then one line on working together. Spell Munsang the same every time.

粵結尾。 「民生大家庭」呢個方向好好。學生、老師、校工、校長分開短句寫，再一句講一齊努力；全文統一拼 Munsang，唔好寫成 Munsung。
