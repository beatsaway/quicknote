Task (2018-style)

Blog — Hong Kong Marathon website

You recently ran the Hong Kong Marathon for the first time.

Write a blog to be posted on the Hong Kong Marathon’s website to encourage other young people to take part in the next marathon.

  • Audience: young people (and anyone reading the official site).
  • Register: positive and persuasive, but still sounds like a real blog, not a formal report.

Draft length: about 310 words · strong length for the task.

Useful angles for this genre first-timer(新手) · community(社群) · atmosphere(氣氛) · route(路線) · iconic (標誌性的) · support team(支援隊) · sign up(報名) · call to action(呼籲行動)

Marking (CLO)

HKDSE Paper 2 is assessed on Content (ideas, relevance, task fulfilment), Language (accuracy, range, tone/register) and Organisation (structure, cohesion, paragraphing). The levels below are indicative only for study—not official HKEAA marks.

Content — Level 4

  • Clear first-timer voice and a sustained attempt to encourage young people to join the next marathon—matches the website blog task.
  • Ideas (route, atmosphere, personal gains, community, all levels) are relevant and build a persuasive case.
  • Strong call to action (sign up, “see you” at the start) rounds off the post well.
  • Could name one concrete detail (e.g. distance you ran, a moment on the course) to push towards Level 5.

Language — Level 3

  • Meaning is usually clear, but grammar, tense, and agreement slip in several places (e.g. helps / has helped, there is/are a team, must try as compound adjective, complete / completion).
  • Some word choice and collocation need polish (full of success, in our online website, lengths to choose and experience) for Level 4.
  • Register is generally right for a lively website blog; higher accuracy would match a public event page better.
  • Local add oil is vivid for HK readers; optional gloss in English for international site visitors (Level 4+ register flexibility).

Organisation — Level 4

  • Clear opening (hook + why join) and logical build from what the event is to benefits, community, inclusivity, and how to sign up.
  • Paragraphing matches stages of the pitch; Also / Furthermore help the line of thought.
  • Watch a few long or fused sentences (comma splices) — and add a bridge sentence before the “connect with people” paragraph—for Level 5 cohesion.

How to do better next time

  • Content: In the opening, address young people directly once (e.g. fellow students / first-time runners) so task engagement is obvious in line 1.
  • Content: Add one specific image or fact (bib, corral, one landmark on the course) so the “I ran” story feels more authentic.
  • Language: After past time markers, check the next clause for tense; proofread articles, prepositions, and agreement with team, crowd, marathon.
  • Language: In lists, keep verbs parallel (e.g. build, improve, make); read aloud for natural collocation on a “read once” web page.
  • Organisation: Between the personal benefits and community sections, one signposting sentence can show how “I gained X” leads to “we run together as a city”.

Overall (indicative): about Level 3–4 — strong content and organisation; language accuracy limits the overall band.

Student draft (blog post)

Last month, I had an unforgettable experience of joining the Hong Kong Marathon for the first time. I can say that this event helps me explore new horizons and can challenge myself. If you are considering taking part in the next marathon, I highly encourage you to join as it is a must try.

Hong Kong Marathon is more than just a race, it is a group activity in our community. The route will take runners to some of the Hong Kong iconic locations or views for example from streets in Kowloon to Victoria harbour. The energy from other runners, the cheers from the crowd and the views around the route create an electrifying atmosphere that motivates you to push your limits.

Also, the marathon gave me an opportunity to learn skills. It helped me build a stronger body, having a better time management and meeting new friends. When I rushed to the finish line, it gave me a feeling of complete. I had finally finished my goal and achieved a good result. I am full of success and happiness.

Furthermore, the marathon is a good way to connect with people in this city. When you are running or crossing the finish line, there are always a support team who will cheer for you in every step. I felt very grateful when I kept hearing people saying add oil.

All levels of runners are able to join the marathon. Even you are a beginner, there are different lengths to choose and experience. You can also get a chance to explore the views of Hong Kong when running. I’m sure that you will get a memorable adventure. Don’t miss the chance to be a part of the marathon. Sign up for the next Hong Kong marathon in our online website and unlock a new achievement together. See you at the starting line!
