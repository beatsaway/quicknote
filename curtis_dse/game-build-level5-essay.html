7. Level 5 model

Should senior secondary students start school later?

Many schools begin early in the morning, and some senior students struggle to focus after too little sleep. While some worry about family schedules and after-school activities, a later start can improve learning conditions. In my view, senior forms should begin later.

First and foremost, a later start can improve students’ focus. For example, teenagers who sleep longer often concentrate better in morning lessons. As a result, both marks and well-being can improve.

Secondly, a later timetable can also reduce morning stress. By starting at nine instead of eight, schools may discourage rushed travel and late arrivals. In turn, more students begin lessons ready to learn.

Critics may argue that a later finish would disrupt family schedules. However, better sleep can raise learning quality, so the extra hour is worthwhile. Therefore, a trial for senior forms is a practical first step.

In conclusion, a later school start time can improve focus and reduce stress. While concerns about schedules are valid, the academic and health benefits are clear. Schools should therefore trial the change for senior forms first.
