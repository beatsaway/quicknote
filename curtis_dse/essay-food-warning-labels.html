The task

Learning English through Debating

To raise public awareness of healthy eating, some countries now legally require food manufacturers to put warning labels on foods that are high in sugar, saturated fat and salt.

Write an argumentative essay. Argue either for OR against the effectiveness of food warning labels in changing people’s eating habits.

  • State your view clearly in the introduction (for or against effectiveness).
  • Give clear reasons with examples; include a short rebuttal if you can.
  • End with a conclusion that echoes your stance.

Draft length (student), about 450 words.

Useful words warning label(警告標籤), saturated fat(飽和脂肪), consumer(消費者), eating habits(飲食習慣), reformulate(重新配方), nutritional(營養的), awareness(意識), policy(政策), discourage(勸阻), accessible(容易獲得的)

Are food warning labels effective?

In recent years, the rise of non-communicable diseases such as obesity, diabetes, and cardiovascular problems has prompted many countries to take measures promoting healthier eating. One prominent strategy is the implementation of food warning labels on products high in sugar, saturated fat, and salt. While some critics argue that these labels are ineffective, evidence suggests that they play a significant role in influencing consumer behavior and encouraging healthier choices. Therefore, I believe that food warning labels are an effective tool in changing people’s eating habits.

First and foremost, food warning labels can act as an immediate signal that alerts customers about the nutrient content of the product. In a market fill with numerous food options, warning labels can provide a quick, simple information, which helps people think whether they want to eat or not. For example, a red warning label ‘high in sugar’ on the package of the candy instantly reminds the risks of health which helps customers to reconsider the purchase. This simple but powerful labels can guide people toward a healthier option and getting a healthier diet.

Secondly, food warning labels can change customers’ lifestyle by raising awareness about the health warnings in some ingredients. When customers see the warnings which say high in sugar or high in fat, it can lead to a better future. Studies from the University of HK have shown that food with warning labels in other countries, such as Japan and Singapore, have experienced a reduce in sales of unhealthy foods. This demonstrates that these labels can effectively push away consumers for choosing unhealthy options, therefore encouraging healthier eating habits.

Furthermore, food warning labels can motivate companies to change their products’ ingredients to avoid negative labelings, creating a healthier food environment. When companies see that their products are labeled as unhealthy, they may try their best to invest in reducing sugar, salt, and saturated fats to improve their company image and overall sales. This can lead to healthier food more widely and easier available and accessible to the public.

Someone may argue that warning labels might not significantly change people’s eating habits. Customers may just ignore the labels. However, changing is a complex process caused by different factors, warning labels are only part of a process. It also needs to include education, pricing policies, and improvement of ingredients. Other countries with warning labels shows that, after including health education and adding different policies, the number of people who eat in a healthier way has increased in these years. Warning labels are the most important part of the healthy lifestyle as they remind people to eat healthy.

To conclude, food warning labels are an effective measure to influence customers choices of buying and promote a healthier eating habit. By providing a clear, visual information about the nutritional content of foods, these labels help customers to make healthier decisions by discouraging the purchase of unhealthy products, and make manufacturers to reformulate their ingredients. While warning labels look like simple, they are an important step towards reducing cases of health issues and fostering a healthier society.
