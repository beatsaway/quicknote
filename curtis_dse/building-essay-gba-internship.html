Question — Greater Bay Area work exposure for senior secondary students

Situation

As Hong Kong deepens links with the Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area, a education bureau discussion paper floats a short, supervised work-exposure module in the GBA for willing Form 5–6 students during long holidays. Supporters mention career horizons; parents worry about safety, cost and lost revision time.

Task

Write an argumentative essay for the school alumni magazine. Should senior secondary students be offered a short, supervised Greater Bay Area work-exposure module? Give reasons and examples to support your view.

  • Write about 500 words.
  • State your view clearly, support it with reasons and examples, and deal with the other side.

Should senior secondary students be offered supervised GBA work exposure?

Career pathways for Hong Kong teenagers are changing with regional links. While families fear lost revision days, short supervised exposure can widen horizons. In my view, senior secondary students should be offered an optional, supervised Greater Bay Area work-exposure module during long holidays.

First and foremost, short exposure can clarify career choices before university. For example, a week in a logistics or design workplace may reveal strengths textbooks never show. As a result, subject and programme choices become more realistic.

Secondly, supervised modules can also build cross-border confidence. By working in mixed teams with clear mentors, students may discourage fear of unfamiliar professional settings. In turn, they return with stronger communication habits.

Critics may argue that the trip wastes revision time and raises safety worries. However, a short optional holiday module with school escorts, insurance and a reflection task can limit risk without eating into term-time lessons. Therefore, a supervised GBA module remains worthwhile.

In conclusion, an optional holiday module can widen careers if safety systems are clear. While revision concerns are valid, keeping it short and supervised makes the offer practical.
