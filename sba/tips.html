Tips for the Assignment

功課貼士

Delivery (Level 5–6)

表達（Level 5–6）

  • Speak clearly, at a steady pace, with natural intonation. 講嘢要清晰，節奏穩定，語調自然。
  • Project your voice without shouting; no artificial aids. 聲量要夠，但唔好嗌；唔可以用擴音器。
  • Use eye contact and body language – face the audience, not the card. 要有眼神交流同肢體語言 – 望住觀眾，唔好成日望咭。
  • Practice so you can present with minimal or no notes. 練熟啲，盡量少用或者唔用筆記。

Content & Structure

內容同結構

  • Have a clear intro, main points, and conclusion. 要有清楚嘅引言、要點同結語。
  • Link ideas: e.g. "This relates to…", "Another example is…" 要連繫 ideas，例如：「This relates to…」、「Another example is…」
  • Elaborate with examples from the film – specific scenes, dialogue, moments. 要用電影嘅例子發揮 – 具體場景、對白、片段。
  • Time yourself: cover the essentials in 8–10 minutes, leave buffer. 計好時間：8–10分鐘講晒重點，留少少 buffer。

What to Avoid

要避免咩

  • Reading from notes – assessors look for dependence on notes. 唔好照讀筆記 – 考官會睇你有冇太依賴筆記。
  • Rushing or going over 12 minutes. 唔好講得太快，都唔好超過12分鐘。
  • Forgetting audience – address them, invite questions if required. 唔好忽略觀眾 – 要同佢哋講嘢，需要時 invite 發問。
  • Memorising long blocks – sounds like "written text spoken aloud". 唔好大段背稿 – 會似讀稿咁，冇自然感。

Cue Card (optional)

提示咭（可選）

  • One 4×6 inch card, both sides allowed. 一張 4×6 吋咭，兩面都可以寫。
  • Use only for bullet points, not full sentences. 只寫要點，唔好寫成句句子。
  • You are advised not to hold it – place it and glance when needed. 建議唔好揸住 – 放喺度，要睇先望一眼。

Guide to Prepare

準備指南

Before the Day

考之前

  1. Watch I See You several times. Note key scenes, characters, and quotes. 睇幾次《I See You》。記低重要場景、角色同對白。
  2. Decide 2–3 main angles: theme, filmmaking, character journey, message. 揀 2–3 個主要角度：主題、電影手法、角色經歷、訊息。
  3. Prepare a structure (intro → points → conclusion) and practice out loud. 準備好結構（引言→要點→結語）同大聲練習。
  4. Build your SBA log with notes, quotes, and your analysis. 寫好 SBA log，包括筆記、引用同你嘅分析。

Procedure on the Day

當日程序

  1. 1:30 p.m. – Report to D506/D507 (preparation room). 下午1:30 – 去 D506/D507 報到（準備室）。
  2. ~30 mins – Silent preparation. SBA log allowed; no iPad or phone. 約30分鐘 – 靜默準備。可以帶 SBA log；唔可以用 iPad 或電話。
  3. Write only brief cue points on your card if you use one. 如果用提示咭，只寫簡短要點。
  4. When called, go to D502 for the assessment. 叫到你之後，去 D502 考。
  5. Recording starts – no restarts. Stay until the lesson ends. 一開錄就冇得重嚟。要留到放堂。

During the 30-Minute Prep

30分鐘準備期間

  • Skim your SBA log and pick the strongest material. 快速睇一次 SBA log，揀最有用嘅料。
  • List 3–5 main points you will cover in order. 列 3–5 個要點，按次序講。
  • Note 1–2 specific examples per point (scene, quote, moment). 每個要點記 1–2 個具體例子（場景、對白、片段）。
  • Plan your opening and closing sentences. 諗定開場同結尾講咩。
