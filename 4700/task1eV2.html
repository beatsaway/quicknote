Task 1: The Bulgari Spa at Bulgari Resort Bali

Fifty-nine villas and mansions sit on the Uluwatu plateau above the Indian Ocean. Within that operation, The Bulgari Spa is positioned as the resort’s flagship wellness product: customized experiences, spa suites, and a menu that merges Indonesian therapeutic tradition with contemporary international spa practice. Brand narrative stresses exclusivity and a holistic healing center identity rather than high-volume day-spa throughput.

Physical identity is immediate: an antique Joglo house, relocated piece by piece from Central Java, forms the reception. Treatment takes place in a cliff-top spa with ocean views. Marketing language describes Traditional Balinese cues alongside Italian contemporary finishing, aligning the spa with the wider Bvlgari hotel aesthetic while keeping the setting (air, light, vegetation, horizon) as part of the therapeutic package.

Bulgari Signature Rituals

Top of the menu are long-format couple and stress-focused journeys, typically delivered in luxurious spa suites. Bulgari Royal Lulur for Two (180 minutes) is built around a historic Javanese palace ritual: foot welcome, rice-and-spice lulur exfoliation, algae body wrap, reflex work on feet and limbs, a cleansing phase, then Biologique Recherche facial work, a plunge pool refresh, and a closing four-hands massage with oils and herbs. Romantic Harmony for Two (180 minutes) stays in-suite for couples: foot ritual, coconut-and-vanilla scrub, Jacuzzi, customized oil massage for moisture and muscular ease, then the same Biologique Recherche facial finish. Stress Recovery (120 minutes) front-loads relaxation with foot ritual, coconut-vanilla scrub, aromatherapy massage, and an express facial for hydration, positioned as detoxifying and firming for skin while resetting mental load.

Body therapies, massage, and facials

Body therapies round out the regional story: Balinese Lulur (bridal-style rice, sandalwood, and spice exfoliation with yoghurt wrap), Balinese Boreh (warming herb paste for circulation and tension), After Sun Soother (coconut scrub plus aloe mask), and an anti-cellulite wrap-and-massage sequence. Massage spans Healing Stones (scrub plus heated volcanic stones and oils), Balinese Four Hands, Herbal Revival (ginger-cinnamon scrub and heated herbal poultices), Thai (no oil, stretching and pressure point), plus traditional, relaxing, and foot-focused oil massage.

Facials lean on Biologique Recherche protocols: diagnosis via Skin Instant© analysis, fragrance-free high-actives, and named menus including brightening and balancing, anti-ageing, after-sun calming, and a men’s purifying track. Hair and nails add a traditional cream bath, shampoo service, manicure, and pedicure so the spa can cover a full grooming arc without sending guests off-property.

Visiting the spa (practical)

Hours: treatments run 9am to 9pm, with last booking at 8pm. Guests are asked to arrive at least 40 minutes early to use facilities. Late arrival shortens treatment time. Robes, towels, and slippers are supplied. Booking goes through spa reservations. Advance reservation is advised, a card may secure the hold, and non-residents may book with minimum notice (property terms apply). The venue asks for quiet, no phones or cameras in treatment areas, and no smoking. Cancellations carry a 24-hour policy on changes to avoid full charge. Minors under 18 require an accompanying adult during treatment.

In-villa options remain available for select accommodations (dedicated spa rooms and pavilions). The main spa, however, carries the full ritual suite, cliff-edge suites, and branded product story described above.
