English

The resort offers three main types of accommodation: single-bed rooms, double-bed rooms, and mansions.

There are 4 varieties of single-bed rooms and 3 varieties of double-bed rooms. The mansions are 2 story buildings with 3 or 5 suites.

Some of these accommodations are situated on cliffs, some close to the sea, and others are tucked away in jungle gardens.

Many feature private pools. The Bvlgari Villas and Mansions additionally offer a private spa, private massage facilities, and even a private gym with a personal trainer.

The Serpenti Villas and Mansions come with full-time private butler service.
