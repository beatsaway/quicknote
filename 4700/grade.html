Resort group project — requirements & grading rubric

Transcribed from subject materials (spagrades). Confirm weightings with your official outline if they differ.

Project weight & role of the report

The group project is worth 35% of total marks. Teams of 5–6 evaluate one resort hotel type covered in the subject, acting as a consulting team for a resort outside Hong Kong and Macau. Work must go beyond facts: apply concepts, critique, and support well-reasoned recommendations.

Required coverage (five areas)

  1. Resort facts and background — accommodation; food and beverage outlets; public areas; leisure or recreational facilities.
  2. Design principles and guidelines applied within the resort.
  3. Target market segments.
  4. Impacts on industry stakeholders from the resort’s facilities, planning, and development.
  5. Specialised topic relevant to the resort type selected — counts toward bonus marks.

Written report — format & submission

  • Word limit: not more than 3,000 words, excluding cover page, executive summary, references, and appendices.
  • Layout: MS Word, A4, 12 pt Times New Roman, 1.5 line spacing, 1" margins all round.
  • Submission: soft copy by email to the subject lecturer by the deadline — no late submission.

Oral presentation

  • Presentation: approximately 25–30 minutes per group.
  • Q&A: about 5–7 minutes.
  • Schedule to be confirmed in class.
