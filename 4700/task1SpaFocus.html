Task 1: Bulgari Resort Bali (spa-led leisure)

Bulgari Resort Bali is a high-end beach resort on the Bukit peninsula at Uluwatu, set above the Indian Ocean with fifty-nine villas and mansions, Italian and Balinese architecture, and a concise mix of restaurants and bars. For this spa-resort brief, the key leisure asset is The Bulgari Spa, marketed as a holistic healing centre that blends Indonesian tradition with up-to-date international spa practice.

The spa reception sits in a relocated Javanese Joglo. Treatment rooms are cliff-top and pair Balinese character with Italian contemporary interiors. Guests are expected about 40 minutes before the appointment so they can use the spa areas without shortening the service (robes, towels, and slippers are supplied). Opening hours are 9am to 9pm, with the last booking at 8pm.

The long signature journeys differ by story and setting. Bulgari Royal Lulur for Two (180 minutes) follows a historic Javanese court narrative in the Royal Spa Pavilion: foot ritual, spiced lulur, algae wrap, reflex work, phases of Biologique Recherche facial care, a plunge pool, then a closing four-hands massage. Romantic Harmony for Two (180 minutes) keeps couples in a private suite with coconut-vanilla scrub, Jacuzzi, bespoke oil massage, and the same facial house style. Stress Recovery (120 minutes) offers scrub, aromatherapy massage, and an express facial for guests who want depth without the full three-hour couples format.

Shorter body treatments include bridal-style Balinese Lulur, warming herbal Boreh, After Sun with aloe, and an anti-cellulite wrap with sculpting massage. Healing Stones, synchronised four-hands work, Herbal Revival poultices, dry Thai massage with stretch, classic Balinese and relaxing oil massage, and reflex-style foot massage cover different uses of heat, pressure, oil, and therapist numbers. Biologique Recherche facials use Skin Instant© analysis. Hair cream bath, manicure, and pedicure keep grooming on site.
