Task 1: Bulgari Resort Bali (spa-led leisure)

Bulgari Resort Bali is a high-end beach resort on the Bukit peninsula at Uluwatu, set above the Indian Ocean with fifty-nine villas and mansions, Italian and Balinese architecture, and a concise mix of restaurants and bars. Food and drink runs from Italian fine dining and wine to all-day service on the cliff and a poolside bar, so guests can eat well without leaving the estate. Landscaped grounds and low villas keep the feel of a private coastal retreat rather than a city high-rise. For this spa-resort brief, the key leisure asset is The Bulgari Spa, marketed as a holistic healing centre that blends Indonesian tradition with up-to-date international spa practice.

The spa reception sits in a relocated Javanese Joglo. Treatment rooms are cliff-top and pair Balinese character with Italian contemporary interiors, with views and natural light folded into the visit. Guests are expected about 40 minutes before the appointment so they can use the spa areas without shortening the service (robes, towels, and slippers are supplied). That pattern matches other multi-night luxury resorts where the spa is treated as part of the main product, not a side stall. Opening hours are 9am to 9pm, with the last booking at 8pm.

The long signature journeys differ by story and setting. Bulgari Royal Lulur for Two (180 minutes) follows a historic Javanese court narrative in the Royal Spa Pavilion: foot ritual, spiced lulur and yoghurt-style polishing, algae wrap, reflex work, phases of Biologique Recherche facial care, a plunge pool, then a closing four-hands massage with rich oils. The sell is ceremonial layering, a cool dip after heat and wrap, and two therapists working in time at the end. Romantic Harmony for Two (180 minutes) keeps couples in a private suite with coconut-vanilla scrub, Jacuzzi, bespoke oil massage, and the same facial house style. It stresses pair privacy and slow hydrotherapy rather than the royal court storyline. Stress Recovery (120 minutes) offers scrub, aromatherapy massage, and an express facial for guests who want depth without the full three-hour couples format.

Shorter body treatments include bridal-style Balinese Lulur (rice, sandalwood, and spice scrub with yoghurt wrap), warming herbal Boreh aimed at heat, circulation, and stiffness, After Sun with coconut scrub and aloe for overexposed skin, and an anti-cellulite wrap combined with deeper sculpting massage. Healing Stones joins scrub with heated volcanic stone work. The dedicated four-hands massage suits guests who find it hard to switch off with one therapist. Herbal Revival uses warm poultices after a ginger and cinnamon scrub. Dry Thai massage uses stretch and pressure through loose clothing with no oil. Classic Balinese, relaxing oil, and reflex-style foot massage round out the list. Biologique Recherche facials use Skin Instant© diagnosis (brightening, anti-ageing, after-sun calming, men’s purifying tracks on the public menu). Hair cream bath, manicure, and pedicure keep grooming on site for longer stays.
