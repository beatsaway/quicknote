Task 1: The Bulgari Spa at Bulgari Resort Bali (short)

Among fifty-nine villas and mansions on the Uluwatu plateau above the Indian Ocean, The Bulgari Spa is positioned as the resort’s lead wellness product: customized experiences in spa suites, a menu joining Indonesian therapeutic tradition with modern international practice, and a brand story built around exclusivity and a holistic healing center rather than high guest throughput. Marketing describes offerings as result-driven yet faithful to regional health and well-being rituals.

Setting and identity

Reception sits inside an antique Joglo house, relocated from Central Java. Treatment space is cliff-top, with ocean views. Materials and detailing pair Balinese references with Italian contemporary style so light, air, and horizon read as part of the treatment, not background decoration.

Signature rituals and wider menu

Long-format journeys anchor the offer. Bulgari Royal Lulur for Two (180 minutes) follows a historic Javanese court sequence: foot ritual, rice-and-spice lulur, algae wrap, reflex work, cleansing, Biologique Recherche facial phases, plunge pool, then a closing four-hands massage. Romantic Harmony for Two (180 minutes) keeps couples in-suite: foot care, coconut-vanilla scrub, Jacuzzi, personalized oil massage, and Biologique Recherche finishing. Stress Recovery (120 minutes) layers foot ritual, scrub, aromatherapy massage, and an express hydrating facial aimed at mental reset and firmer, cleaner skin.

Body therapies include Balinese Lulur, warming Boreh, After Sun Soother, and anti-cellulite body work. Massage runs from Healing Stones and herbal poultice “Revival” to four-hands, Thai, traditional Balinese, relaxing, and foot massage. Facials use Biologique Recherche protocols and Skin Instant© diagnosis. Hair and nails add cream bath, manicure, and pedicure for a single-venue grooming loop.

Practical information

Treatments operate 9am to 9pm. Last booking 8pm. Arrive at least 40 minutes early or time may be shortened. Robes and slippers are provided. Reserve through spa reservations. A credit card may be required to secure bookings, and non-residents should confirm minimum advance notice under hotel policy. Mention health conditions, allergies, or injuries when booking so therapists can adapt safely. Keep voices low. No phones or cameras in the spa. No smoking. Changes or cancellations need 24 hours’ notice to avoid full charge. Guests under 18 must have an adult present. Select villas offer in-room or pavilion treatments, but the main spa holds the full ritual suite and cliff-edge suites.
