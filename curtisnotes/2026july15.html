6 predicted Part B themes · L5 / GCSE Gr9 demos

Task

Topic A · Essay / school magazine

Generative AI and schoolwork

Generative AI tools such as ChatGPT are now easy for secondary students to use. Some teachers welcome them as learning aids. Others worry that AI will encourage cheating and weaken students’ writing and thinking skills.

Write an essay for a school magazine giving your view on whether secondary students should be allowed to use generative AI for schoolwork.

  • State your view clearly.
  • Support it with two or three developed reasons and examples.
  • Respond briefly to the main opposing argument.

Practice stance: allow AI as a support tool, with clear school rules — not unchecked copying.

Why 2027: AI literacy is already in classrooms and public debate.

Topic B · Letter / article

Later school start times

Research suggests that teenagers need more sleep, yet many Hong Kong secondary schools start before 8 a.m. Some parents want later start times. Others worry about transport, after-school lessons and family schedules.

Write a letter to your principal recommending a short trial of later school start times.

  • Explain why sleep matters for learning and health.
  • Address at least one practical concern.
  • Propose a realistic trial plan.

Practice stance: support a limited trial (e.g. start 30–45 minutes later).

Why 2027: sleep, mental health and campus wellbeing keep returning in Paper 2.

Topic C · Speech

Short-form video and free time

Many teenagers spend hours daily on short-form video apps. Some say these platforms inspire creativity and help students relax. Critics argue that endless scrolling damages concentration, sleep and real-life relationships.

Write a speech for a school assembly. Persuade classmates to set healthier limits on short-form video use.

  • Give clear reasons for setting limits.
  • Suggest practical habits students can try.
  • End with a call to action.

Practice stance: not “delete forever” — promote sensible daily limits.

Why 2027: digital wellbeing / media literacy is a strong modern argument theme.

Topic D · Article / speech

Mental health education in schools

More Hong Kong students report stress and anxiety, yet some schools still treat mental health as a private family matter. Supporters want regular wellbeing lessons and easier access to counselling. Critics say the curriculum is already full and that such topics may worry students further.

Write an article for your school magazine arguing whether secondary schools should make mental health education a regular part of school life.

  • State your view clearly.
  • Give developed reasons with school-based examples.
  • Respond briefly to one concern.

Practice stance: yes — regular, practical education (not one-off talks only).

Why 2027: student wellbeing and counselling access stay high on the education agenda.

Topic E · Letter / proposal

Compulsory money skills

Many Form 6 leavers start university or work with little confidence in budgeting, saving or spotting online scams. Some educators want financial literacy to be a compulsory secondary-school module. Others argue that parents should teach money skills at home.

Write a letter to the Education Bureau recommending compulsory financial literacy lessons in secondary schools.

  • Explain why school teaching is needed.
  • Give practical topic examples.
  • Address the “leave it to parents” objection.

Practice stance: support a short compulsory module for all forms.

Why 2027: scams, online shopping and early money decisions are everyday student realities.

Topic F · School magazine essay

Study abroad or stay in Hong Kong?

Some families see overseas university study as the best path to a global career. Others argue that staying in Hong Kong offers strong local networks, lower cost and equally good prospects. The debate remains common among Form 6 students.

Write an essay for a young people’s magazine. Give your view on whether studying abroad is always better than staying in Hong Kong for university.

  • Take a clear stance (not “it depends” with no decision).
  • Support your view with two or three developed reasons.
  • Respond briefly to the opposite view.

Practice stance for demos: staying in Hong Kong can be equally beneficial — not always weaker.

Why 2027: JUPAS / overseas offers remain a classic high-frequency Paper 2 theme.

Topic A — Generative AI

粵語 = 英文 demo 翻譯。試寫成英文，再 ▶。

A1 · Introduction

近年，生成式人工智能喺中學引起咗激烈討論。有啲老師視佢為有用嘅學習輔助工具，但亦有人擔心佢會鼓勵抄襲，並削弱學生嘅思考能力。我相信應該容許學生用 AI 做功課，前提係學校訂下清晰指引，而且學生仍然要交出自己嘅作品。
has sparked heated debate、 learning aid、 undermine、 I am convinced that、 provided that、 clear guidelines

Demo · A intro In recent years, generative AI has sparked heated debate in secondary schools. Some teachers see it as a useful learning aid, while others fear it will encourage plagiarism and undermine students’ thinking skills. I am convinced that students should be allowed to use AI for schoolwork, provided that schools set clear guidelines and students still produce work of their own.
A2 · Body 1 · guided learning

首先，從學術角度來看，AI 可以幫助學生拆解困難概念，並即時得到有建設性嘅回饋。一個清楚例子係用 AI 工具解釋一篇較難嘅閱讀篇章，然後用自己文字重寫重點。咁用嘅話，AI 係支援理解，而唔係用來抄襲。
First and foremost、 from an academic perspective、 break down difficult concepts、 A clear example is、 rather than copying

Demo · A body 1 First and foremost, from an academic perspective, AI can help students break down difficult concepts and receive quick, constructive feedback. A clear example is using an AI tool to explain a dense reading passage, then rewriting the main points in one’s own words. Used in this way, AI supports understanding rather than copying.
A3 · Body 2 · future readiness

其次，實際上嚟講，AI 工具喺大學同工作場所越來越普遍。如果學校全面禁止，學生可能中學畢業後都唔識負責任咁使用。以大學專題為例：好多課程已期望學生能妥善運用數碼工具，所以及早培養數碼素養好重要。
Secondly、 Practically speaking、 increasingly common、 responsible use、 Take … as a case in point

Demo · A body 2 Secondly, practically speaking, AI tools are becoming increasingly common in universities and workplaces. If schools ban them completely, students may leave secondary education without learning responsible use. Take university project work as a case in point: many courses already expect students to use digital tools wisely, so early training in digital literacy is essential.
A4 · Rebuttal

批評者可能認為 AI 只會令作弊更容易，並削弱學生寫作。呢個擔心雖然可以理解，但可以透過學校政策處理。可以要求學生申報有冇用 AI，而關鍵評核仍然可以喺課堂限時完成，喺冇工具幫助下測試真正能力。
Critics may argue that、 While this concern is understandable、 it can be addressed by、 declare AI assistance、 under timed conditions

Demo · A rebuttal Critics may argue that AI will simply make cheating easier and weaken students’ writing. While this concern is understandable, it can be addressed by school policy. Students can be required to declare AI assistance, and key assessments can still be completed under timed conditions in class, where genuine ability is tested without tools.
A5 · Conclusion

總括而言，生成式 AI 既唔應該被禁止，亦唔應該被無視。因此我建議中學教學生點樣負責任咁使用，同時堅持要誠實、獨立完成作業。有清晰規則，AI 可以加強學習，而唔係取而代之。
To conclude、 neither ban nor ignore、 I therefore recommend、 honest independent work

Demo · A conclusion To conclude, generative AI should be neither banned nor ignored. I therefore recommend that secondary schools teach students how to use it responsibly, while still insisting on honest, independent work. With clear rules, AI can strengthen learning instead of replacing it.

Topic B — Later school start times

粵語 = 英文 demo 翻譯。試寫成英文，再 ▶。

B1 · Opening + purpose

校長您好：我寫信係想建議短期試行較遲嘅上課時間。我哋好多人八點前已開始上課，但青少年往往需要多過而家得到嘅睡眠。一個仔細計劃嘅改變，可以同時改善學習同身心健康。
I am writing to recommend、 a short trial、 need more sleep、 before 8 a.m.

Demo · B opening Dear Principal,

I am writing to recommend a short trial of later school start times. Many of us begin lessons before 8 a.m., yet teenagers often need more sleep than they currently get. A carefully planned change could improve both learning and wellbeing.
B2 · Body 1 · learning & health

首先，從健康同學習角度來看，睡眠直接影響警覺、記性同情緒。一個清楚例子係每日第一堂：學生攰到嚟校，好難專注，又易犯本可避免嘅錯誤。就算遲半小時開課，都可能幫更多人帶住狀態嚟學。
First and foremost、 from a health and learning perspective、 alertness、 A clear example is、 ready to learn

Demo · B body 1 First and foremost, from a health and learning perspective, sleep directly affects alertness, memory and mood. A clear example is the first lesson of the day: when students arrive exhausted, they struggle to concentrate and make avoidable mistakes. Starting even 30 minutes later could help more of us arrive ready to learn.
B3 · Body 2 · safer routines

其次，實際上嚟講，較遲開課亦可以令早上更安全、更從容。例如，而家要摸黑出門趕早車嘅學生，會有多啲時間小心行路同食早餐，而唔使半醒咁趕過全日。
Secondly、 Practically speaking、 safer and calmer morning routine、 For example、 half-awake

Demo · B body 2 Secondly, practically speaking, a later start could also create a safer and calmer morning routine. For example, students who currently leave home in the dark to catch early transport would have a little more time to travel carefully and eat breakfast, instead of rushing through the day half-awake.
B4 · Rebuttal + trial plan

有啲家長可能擔心交通接駁、補習同家庭時間表。呢個顧慮係合理嘅，但半年試行可以令學校小心調動時間表，並喺永久決定前收集意見。放學時間只要同步延遲同樣幅度就得。
Some parents may worry that、 While this concern is fair、 a six-month trial、 collect feedback、 Dismissal times

Demo · B rebuttal Some parents may worry about transport links, tutorial classes and family schedules. While this concern is fair, a six-month trial would allow the school to adjust the timetable carefully and collect feedback before making any permanent decision. Dismissal times could simply shift later by the same amount.
B5 · Closing

希望你會為學生健康同學業進度考慮呢個建議。短期試行可以畀我哋實證，而唔係空估。謝謝你嘅時間。
此致
Chris Wong
中五丙
I hope you will consider、 in the best interests of、 evidence rather than speculation、 Yours sincerely

Demo · B closing I hope you will consider this proposal in the best interests of students’ health and academic progress. A short trial would give us evidence rather than speculation. Thank you for your time.

Yours sincerely,
Chris Wong
Form 5C

Topic C — Short-form video limits (speech)

粵語 = 英文 demo 翻譯。試寫成英文，再 ▶。

C1 · Opening

各位早晨。短片 Apps 的確好玩，我今晚唔係叫大家刪走佢哋。我係想大家訂更健康嘅使用限制，因為不停刷片正靜靜咁消耗我哋嘅睡眠、專注同真正嘅空閒時間。
Good morning、 I am not asking you to delete、 set healthier limits、 endless scrolling

Demo · C opening Good morning, everyone. Short-form video apps can be entertaining, and I am not asking you to delete them tonight. I am asking you to set healthier limits, because endless scrolling is quietly costing us sleep, focus and real free time.
C2 · Body 1 · concentration

首先，從學術角度來看，呢啲 Apps 訓練我哋大腦期待短暫嘅娛樂刺激。咁會令課堂同溫習時更難長時間專注。一個好熟嘅例子係溫書時開 App「只睇一條」，然後半個鐘先察覺。
First and foremost、 from an academic perspective、 short bursts of entertainment、 sustained concentration、 A familiar example is

Demo · C body 1 First and foremost, from an academic perspective, these apps train our brains to expect short bursts of entertainment. That makes sustained concentration harder in class and during revision. A familiar example is opening an app for “just one video” during homework and losing half an hour before we notice.
C3 · Body 2 · sleep & mood

其次，喺身心健康方面，臨瞓前刷片往往損害睡眠質素。例如，我哋好多人都睇到半夜過後，第二朝就攰同易嬲。睡前設更好限制，可以保護休息同情緒。
Secondly、 In terms of wellbeing、 late-night scrolling、 sleep quality、 irritable

Demo · C body 2 Secondly, in terms of wellbeing, late-night scrolling often damages sleep quality. For example, many of us keep watching until well after midnight, then feel tired and irritable the next morning. Better limits before bedtime would protect both rest and mood.
C4 · Practical habits + soft rebuttal

當然，呢啲 Apps 唔係全部都有害；有啲短片可以學技能或者幫人放鬆。真正問題係失控使用。我哋可以設每日螢幕時間上限，溫書時開 focus mode，同埋至少瞓前一小時停止刷片。細習慣比突然全面禁止、又守唔到更有用。
Of course, these apps are not all harmful、 The real issue is、 screen-time limits、 focus mode、 an hour before bed

Demo · C habits Of course, these apps are not all harmful; some clips can teach skills or help us unwind. The real issue is uncontrolled use. We can set daily screen-time limits, switch on focus mode during revision, and stop scrolling at least an hour before bed. Small habits matter more than sudden bans we cannot keep.
C5 · Call to action

總括而言，短片好好玩，但只限於我哋仍然掌控佢。我呼籲大家呢個星期揀一個實際每日限額，然後堅持。一齊奪回我哋嘅時間、睡眠同注意力。多謝。
To conclude、 I urge you、 choose a realistic daily limit、 take back control、 Thank you

Demo · C closing To conclude, short-form video is enjoyable, but only if we stay in charge of it. I urge you to choose a realistic daily limit this week and stick to it. Let’s take back control of our time, our sleep and our attention. Thank you.

Topic D — Mental health education

粵語 = 英文 demo 翻譯。試寫成英文，再 ▶。

D1 · Introduction

香港中學生嘅壓力已到咗難以忽視嘅地步。有啲人仍然當精神健康係私人家庭事務，但我相信學校應將精神健康教育列為常規校園生活一部分，而唔係只靠一次集會講座。
has become impossible to ignore、 a private family matter、 I am convinced that、 a regular part of school life

Demo · D intro Stress among Hong Kong secondary students has become impossible to ignore. Some people still treat mental health as a private family matter, but I am convinced that schools should make mental health education a regular part of school life, not a one-off assembly talk.
D2 · Body 1 · early support

首先，從身心健康角度來看，常規課堂幫助學生識別壓力早期警號，並知道點樣求助。一個清楚例子係考試季節：如果學生喺問題惡化前已明白焦慮同輔導選擇，佢哋較少會默默受苦。
First and foremost、 from a wellbeing perspective、 early warning signs、 A clear example is、 exam season

Demo · D body 1 First and foremost, from a wellbeing perspective, regular lessons help students recognise early warning signs of stress and know how to seek help. A clear example is exam season: if students understand anxiety and counselling options before problems escalate, they are less likely to suffer in silence.
D3 · Body 2 · reduce stigma

其次，喺校園文化方面，公開討論可以減少污名。例如，當老師用正常、尊重嘅語氣講輔導時，學生較唔會覺得求助係弱嘅表現。呢種轉變同任何工作紙一樣重要。
Secondly、 In terms of school culture、 reduce stigma、 a sign of weakness、 For example

Demo · D body 2 Secondly, in terms of school culture, open discussion can reduce stigma. For example, when teachers talk about counselling in a normal, respectful way, students are less likely to see asking for help as a sign of weakness. That shift matters as much as any worksheet.
D4 · Rebuttal

批評者可能認為課程已經太擠迫，又或者講精神健康會令學生更擔心。呢個顧慮雖然可以理解，但短而實用嘅單元唔一定要取代核心科目。清晰指引通常會令學生安心，而唔係嚇親佢哋。
Critics may argue that、 overcrowded curriculum、 While this concern is understandable、 short practical modules

Demo · D rebuttal Critics may argue that the curriculum is already overcrowded, or that talking about mental health will make students worry more. While this concern is understandable, short practical modules need not replace core subjects. Clear guidance usually reassures students rather than frightening them.
D5 · Conclusion

總括而言，精神健康教育應該屬於中學校園生活。因此我建議有常規、實用嘅課堂，同更易接觸輔導服務，令學生可以同時保護身心健康同學習。
To conclude、 I therefore recommend、 regular and practical、 easier access to counselling

Demo · D conclusion To conclude, mental health education belongs in secondary school life. I therefore recommend regular, practical lessons and easier access to counselling, so students can protect both their wellbeing and their learning.

Topic E — Compulsory money skills

粵語 = 英文 demo 翻譯。試寫成英文，再 ▶。

E1 · Opening

先生／女士您好：我寫信建議中學設必修理財教育課。好多學生離開學校時都能合格考試，卻唔肯定點樣編預算、儲蓄，或者認出網上騙案。
I am writing to recommend、 compulsory financial literacy、 budget, save、 online scams

Demo · E opening Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to recommend compulsory financial literacy lessons in secondary schools. Many students leave school able to pass exams, yet unsure how to budget, save or spot online scams.
E2 · Body 1 · real-life need

首先，實際上嚟講，學生一開始工作或者上大學，金錢技能就影響日常決定。一個清楚例子係第一份兼職人工：如果冇基本預算能力，好易喺生活開支上使過度，之後連交通或者課本費都吃力。
First and foremost、 Practically speaking、 everyday decisions、 A clear example is、 first part-time wage

Demo · E body 1 First and foremost, practically speaking, money skills affect everyday decisions as soon as students start work or university. A clear example is a first part-time wage: without basic budgeting, it is easy to overspend on lifestyle costs and then struggle with transport or course materials.
E3 · Body 2 · equal access

其次，喺公平方面，唔係每個家庭都有同樣信心或者時間教理財。一個短暫必修單元可以畀所有學生平等接觸呢項核心生活技能，而唔使靠運氣。
Secondly、 In terms of fairness、 not every family、 equal access、 core life skill

Demo · E body 2 Secondly, in terms of fairness, not every family can teach money management with the same confidence or time. A short compulsory module would give all students equal access to a core life skill, instead of leaving it to chance.
E4 · Rebuttal

有人認為金錢技能應喺屋企教。父母雖然仍然重要，但學校可以涵蓋家庭未必解釋得好嘅現代風險，例如釣魚短訊、假投資 App，同埋不安全嘅網購習慣。
Some people argue that、 should be taught at home、 While parents remain important、 schools can cover

Demo · E rebuttal Some people argue that money skills should be taught at home. While parents remain important, schools can cover modern risks that households may not explain well, such as phishing messages, fake investment apps and unsafe online shopping habits.
E5 · Closing

希望教育局會考慮為中學生設短暫必修理財單元。更好金錢技能可以幫年輕人帶住更大信心、更少代價高昂錯誤進入成年。
此致
Alex Chan
中五學生
I hope the Bureau will consider、 a short compulsory module、 enter adulthood、 Yours faithfully

Demo · E closing I hope the Bureau will consider a short compulsory financial literacy module for secondary students. Better money skills would help young people enter adulthood with more confidence and fewer costly mistakes.

Yours faithfully,
Alex Chan
Secondary 5 student

Topic F — Study abroad vs stay in Hong Kong

粵語 = 英文 demo 翻譯。試寫成英文，再 ▶。

F1 · Introduction

到海外升學經常被講成有志向中六生嘅最佳出路。雖然海外讀書可以好有價值，但我唔同意佢一定更好。好多情況下，喺香港讀大學同樣有益。
is often presented as、 the best path、 I disagree that、 equally beneficial

Demo · F intro Studying abroad is often presented as the best path for ambitious Form 6 students. While overseas study can be valuable, I disagree that it is always better. In many cases, staying in Hong Kong for university can be equally beneficial.
F2 · Body 1 · networks & opportunity

首先，從事業角度來看，喺香港升學幫學生及早建立本地網絡。一個清楚例子係暑假實習：學生可以見僱主、參與本地項目、了解就業市場，又唔使承受海外生活額外成本同簽證限制。
First and foremost、 from a career perspective、 local networks、 A clear example is、 internships

Demo · F body 1 First and foremost, from a career perspective, studying in Hong Kong helps students build local networks early. A clear example is summer internships: students can meet employers, join local projects and understand the job market without the extra cost and visa limits of living overseas.
F3 · Body 2 · cost & family

其次，實際上嚟講，留港可以減輕家庭財務壓力。海外學費同生活費往往高好多。例如，留喺本地升學嘅學生可能少啲擔心債務，更能穩定專注學位。
Secondly、 Practically speaking、 financial pressure、 tuition and living costs、 For example

Demo · F body 2 Secondly, practically speaking, staying in Hong Kong can reduce financial pressure on families. Tuition and living costs abroad are often far higher. For example, students who remain local may worry less about debt and focus more steadily on their degree.
F4 · Rebuttal

支持海外升學嘅人認為，只有住喺外地先有真正全球視野。呢個好處雖然真實，但唔係唯一途徑。好多香港大學都有交換計劃、國際同學同暑期海外課程，因此學生仍可獲得更廣經驗，而唔使整個學位都出國。
Supporters of overseas study argue that、 global exposure、 While this benefit is real、 exchange programmes

Demo · F rebuttal Supporters of overseas study argue that only living abroad gives true global exposure. While this benefit is real, it is not the only route. Many Hong Kong universities offer exchange programmes, international classmates and overseas summer schools, so students can still gain wider experience without studying abroad for the full degree.
F5 · Conclusion

總括而言，出國升學唔係自動更優勝。對好多中六生嚟講，留喺香港仍然係穩健而明智嘅選擇，一樣可以支持志向、機會同個人成長。
To conclude、 not automatically superior、 a strong and sensible choice

Demo · F conclusion To conclude, studying abroad is not automatically superior. For many Form 6 students, staying in Hong Kong remains a strong and sensible choice that still supports ambition, opportunity and personal growth.

Self-check before you finish

  • Stance 清唔清晰？有冇條件式立場（e.g. allow AI with rules）？
  • 每段有冇具體例子（唔得淨係 happier / interested）？
  • 有冇短 rebuttal？
  • 用詞自然未？寧願準確短句，唔好硬塞生僻字。
