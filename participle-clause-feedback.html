分詞子句練習 — 老師回饋

整體表現：你已能正確運用 Having + p.p.、現在分詞 與 Being + p.p. 等結構；下列是逐題重點與可改進處。

一、句子合併（Participle clause）

🌲📚🎓 考完試／離開教室 正確

Having finished their exam, the students left the classroom quietly.

主從分明，完成式分詞用得恰當。

🌲🚗💰 車禍／報警 正確

Seeing the car accident, the man immediately called the police.

現在分詞表「同時／一先一後」的附屬動作，沒問題。

🌲🏠🔥 火災／重建 請修正

Begin damaged by the fire, …

Begin 是打字錯誤，應為 Being（被動 + 附屬說明）。

Being damaged by the fire, the house had to be rebuilt.

🌲👧🍦 冰淇淋／掉在地上 正確

Being excited about her ice cream, the little girl dropped it on the ground.

結構清楚。

🌲🍽 主廚／上菜 正確

Having prepared the meal, the chef presented it to the guests with pride.

「先準備、再呈現」，Having + p.p. 很合適。

🌲😴😡 累／哭 正確

Being tired, the baby cried loudly.

狀態用 Being + 形容詞，正確。

🌲📝 老師／A+ 正確

Being impressed by my essay, the teacher gave me an A+.

被動意義清楚。

🌲🍝🍴 義大利麵／提拉米蘇 請修正

Being finished our pasta, …

Being finished 在此不自然；主詞是「我們」主動吃完，應用 Having finished（先吃完，再點甜點）。

Having finished our pasta, we ordered some tiramisu for dessert.

🌲🌧 走路回家／撿錢 正確

Walking home, she found a $100 bill on the ground.

很好。

🌲🖼 畫被偷／尋回 可更自然

Being stolen from the museum, it was later found …

被動開頭時，英語常直接用過去分詞 Stolen …，不必加 Being（Being stolen 較少見、聽起來較怪）。也可寫成 Having been stolen（較正式）。

Stolen from the museum, the painting was later found in an abandoned house.

二、改寫句子

🌲🐶🦴 狗／句首 請修正

Being waked by a strange noise, …

動詞 wake 的過去分詞是 woken，不是 waked。也可用 Woken by … 開頭（不加 Being）。

Woken by a strange noise, the dog started to bark.
（或：Being woken by a strange noise, the dog started to bark.）

🌲☕ Amy／咖啡 大致正確

Amy scrolled through her feed, sipping her coffee.

分詞放句末、表伴隨動作，很好。若題目要保留原意，可寫 social media feed。

📚 名作家／暢銷書（句中） 請修正

The book, being written by a famous author, became a bestseller.

書「已寫成」是完成狀態，不宜用 being written（像「正在被寫」）。改寫關係子句時通常用 written by …。

The book, written by a famous author, became a bestseller.

🌲🚌 趕公車／跌倒 正確

Running to catch the bus, he tripped on the sidewalk.

沒問題。

🌲🎵💃 Sarah／音樂 小修正

Sarah danced around her room, Listening to her favorite music.

句中分詞 listening 不必大寫 L。兩個動作同時發生，結構正確。

Sarah danced around her room, listening to her favorite music.

🌲🌧☔ 忘傘／淋濕 正確

Forgetting her umbrella, she got completely soaked.

精簡又清楚。

🌲👶🛌 累／睡著 正確

Being exhausted after a long day, the baby fell asleep instantly.

很好。

三、翻譯練習

🌱 沒收到訊息／擔心 未完成／請補齊

（句首留空）, he started to worry.

建議：Not receiving her message(s) 或 Not having received her message(s)。

Not receiving her messages, he started to worry.

🌱 聲音／加快腳步 正確

Hearing a strange noise behind her, she walked faster.

正確。

🌱 2019年出版／暢銷 可更自然

Being published in 2019, the book quickly became a bestseller.

書籍已出版，常用 Published in 2019, … 或 First published in 2019, …。Being published 較少見。

Published in 2019, the book quickly became a bestseller.

🌱 厭倦工作／辭職 請補完整

Being bored, he decided to quit his job.

宜寫出「對什麼感到厭倦」：Bored with his job 或 Being bored with his job。

Bored with his job, he decided to quit.

🌿 國外十年／回台 正確

After living overseas for 10 years, he decided to move back to Taiwan.

After + V-ing 很清楚（也可用 Having lived …）。

🌿 兒子的話／感動 用字可調整

Being touched by her son’s conversation, …

conversation 可改為 words 或 what her son said，較符合「被孩子的話感動」。

Deeply touched by her son’s words, the mother couldn’t help but cry.

🌿 錢包被偷／付錢 請補齊

（句首留空）, he couldn’t pay.

可用：His wallet (having been) stolen 或 With his wallet stolen。

His wallet stolen, he couldn’t pay.

🌿 作業／出去玩 請修正

Finishing her homework, Amy went out with her friends.

「先做完作業再出去」宜強調先後：Having finished her homework。

Having finished her homework, Amy went out with her friends.

🌿 遺棄的車／拖走 題目要求分詞片語

The car that was abandoned on the street was towed away yesterday.

文法沒錯，但應把關係子句改為分詞片語放名詞後：abandoned on the street。

The car abandoned on the street was towed away yesterday.

🌲 咖啡廳／Latte 正確

She sat in the café, drinking the latte, looking at the crowds outside.

分詞並列自然。

🌲 背叛／傑克 代名詞

Being betrayed by her best friend, Jack felt heartbroken …

Jack 是男性，應為 his best friend。

Betrayed by his best friend, Jack felt heartbroken, not knowing who he should trust.

Bonus 功課／跑出去玩 可更精準

Finishing their homework, the children ran out to play, ignoring …

與「做完功課後」的時間先後，用 Having finished their homework 更穩妥。

Bonus 屋子燒毀／站著 請修正結構

The house was burnt, he stood there, Not knowing …

兩個完整子句不可只逗號連接（comma splice）。宜改為分詞／with 結構；Not knowing 不必大寫；「下一步」是 what to do next。

His house (having been) burnt down, he stood there, not knowing what to do next.

Bonus 紀錄片／塑膠 大致正確

Seeing an environmental documentary …, he began to change …, trying to use less plastic products.

若題目有「深受感動」，可加上 Deeply moved, … 或 Moved by …, …。整體可接受。

Bonus 月台／揮旗 正確

He stood on the platform, waving a flag, leading the train to the station slowly.

結構清楚；slowly 也可放在 leading 前面。

Bonus 長日工作／沙發 小修正

Finishing a long day’s work … only wanting to lay on the couch and do nothings.

先後關係可用 Having finished；「躺」在沙發上常用 lie（lay 易混淆）；do nothing 不可加 s。

Having finished a long day’s work, he dragged his tired body home, only wanting to lie on the couch and do nothing.

總複習提醒

  • Having + p.p.：附屬動作「先發生、已完成」。
  • 現在分詞 V-ing：主動、同時或附屬進行。
  • 過去分詞：被動或已完成狀態；開頭常用 Stolen … / Written … / Published …，不必勉強加 Being。
  • Being + p.p./adj.：狀態或被動，但別和 woken / finished（主動完成） 搞混。
  • 避免 comma splice：兩個完整句子不能只靠逗號連接。
