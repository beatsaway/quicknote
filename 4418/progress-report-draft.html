1) Title of the Study

How the Rise of AI Mental Support Chatbots Affects Employee Job Engagement

2) Objectives of the Study

  • To examine whether employees’ AI mental health chatbot usage is associated with higher job engagement (vigour, dedication, absorption).
  • To test whether psychological distress mediates the relationship between chatbot usage and job engagement.
  • To explore whether factors such as usage frequency, perceived usefulness, and organisational support strengthen or weaken the relationship.
