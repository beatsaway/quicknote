What went well

Funny heat images + a clear mini-adventure (wrong station, wrong date, ice bag).

  • 🍳 Voice. Cook-an-egg hair and “like I’m cooked” feel like a real blog.
  • 🏉 Story arc. Early start → sleep on the train → Ho Man Tin → empty pitch → Starbucks.
  • 🌡️ Theme. You return to extreme HK weather at the end — nice frame.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Spelling. stepped, rugby, except, Ho Man Tin.
  • 💡 Past tense. Story day verbs: was, skipped, had to, was shocked.
  • 💡 Articles. a few weeks, no people, a few older children.
  • 💡 Clear subjects. “suddenly shocked” needs I was.

OMG! The weather in HK is crazy

Hey guys,

Sorry for not updating my blog entry for few weeks because this few weeks are very hot that I didn’t hang out. When I steped out a small step from my home, my body is sweating everywhere and like your hair can even cook an egg!

Yesterday, it's the first day that my younger brother, Nathan, played rubigy. Therefore I woke up very early to go to King’s Park Sports Ground. During the ride to there. I was so sleepy so I fell asleep. Unfortunately, father had been a long time not going there so we skip one station! The station that we dropped off is Ho Man fin station! So we have to run there to avoid late.

After 15-20 minutes, when we arrived there, there were none people, excect a coach and few older children. When my father asked the coach, the coach said the mini group will start the 1st lesson on 31st of August! suddenly shocked and even can't say a word! Because at that moment, I've been sweated a lot like an ice melting! Luckily, there had ice take to cool down ourselves so I took a lot of them and a plastic bag. When the plastic bag touched down me, “Wow!” it really cooled!

Later. We went to Starbucks to rest for a while then we rode back train to back home. The weather in HK is very extreme now! When it’s sunny, it’s really extremely hot like I’m cooked! When it’s rainy, it rains a lot! I really hate the extreme weather in HK!
