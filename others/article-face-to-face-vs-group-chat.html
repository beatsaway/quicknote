Magazine article (draft)

In today's world, social interactions take place in the physical and digital world, and each has its own roles. Face-to-face meetings allow you to read non-verbal cues, and an intimacy that is hard to achieve through screens. Meanwhile, group chats provide convenience and inclusivity, bridging distances, enabling friends to share moments instantly, ensuring no one feels left out.

Texting, however, can lead to misunderstandings due to lack of tone and this has led to the use of emojis and GIFs to express emotional nuances. In the end, difficult conversations are best done face-to-face because they allow for the kind of focus and connection that's hard to replicate with digital distractions. Digital platforms have their place for casual interactions, but the important moments are best enjoyed in the real world, where real support and real presence matter most.
