Opinion. The reason why I love Halloween

Topic. The reason why I love Halloween

Give the reasons why you love Halloween. Explain each one with an example, then end with a sentence that sums up how you feel.

  • Success criteria. Three clear reasons. Each one explained. A closing sentence that says what the day means to you.
  • Only short yellow phrases are suggested edits; the rest is the draft as written.
  • Click flow. Yellow → green + comment. Only one comment at a time. Click the comment box, elsewhere, or green again to hide it. Click green with comment open to go back to yellow.
Useful words cosplay, dress up, play tricks on someone, trick or treat, silly photos, get away with, entrance, unforgettable, school hall, ton of candy

What went well

Three reasons, one per paragraph, and each comes with a story that only you could tell.

  • 🎯 Clear reasons. The first, second and third reasons are easy to follow, so the reader never loses the thread.
  • 😂 Funny detail. Emoji photos in the toilet and a teacher who never caught you make the writing feel real.
  • 🎭 Concrete examples. Shoes, clowns and pumpkins as costumes are pictures, not just labels.
  • 🗣️ Your own voice. Short exclamations and the dare to trick your friends sound like you talking.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Spelling. teens, funny, candies, entrance. Four words to learn by heart.
  • 💡 Reason sentences. The first reason I love Halloween is that, not reason why … because.
  • 💡 Although. Although this year I have no time to go to Ocean Park, I can still dress up at school. One sentence, one comma.
  • 💡 Word choice. abstracted means deep in thought. For a strange costume, say weird.
  • 💡 Title. You give three reasons, so the title wants the plural, reasons.
  • 💡 Last line. Add one wish for next year, such as a plan to go to Ocean Park at last.

The reason why I love... Halloween.

Most of the people in Hong Kong don't celebrate Halloween seriously like Chinese New Year and Christmas. However there are also some people, especially, teeps, like me who love to celebrate Halloween!

The first reason why I love Halloween because I can play tricks on my friends, and they won't report to teachers! We will play tricks with each others. One the most is that we use our phone after school and go to toilet to take people's silly photos and make with emojis. It was really farmy! fortunately teachers didn't find us playing phones in the toilet.

The second reason is that I can cosplay different abstracted characters, for example, shoes, clowns and pumpkins. Although this year I have no time to go to Ocean Park to play. I can play it in the school. My class teacher said we were crazy but was. an unforgettable memory.

The third reason is because I can get a ton of candices at school by "tricks or treats! Our school will celebrate Halloween and give out candices in the morning at the school enterance every year. If my classmates or friends don't give candices I will trick. them same as my friends too!

Halloween let me create a lot of unforgettable memories so I love it a lot.

Comments

Three reasons, three stories, one clear voice. The fixes are mostly spelling and sentence shapes, so the ideas can stand out.

ENClear order. First, second, third, one paragraph each. A reader can find every reason without hunting.

粵次序清楚。第一、第二、第三，每段一個原因，讀者一眼就搵到。

ENReal stories. The emoji photos and the candy at the gate are moments a reader remembers, not just reasons.

粵故事真實。廁所影相、門口派糖，係讀者記得嘅畫面，唔止係理由。

ENYour voice. Exclamations and the dare to trick your friends sound like you, which makes the piece fun to read.

粵有你嘅聲音。感嘆號同「唔派糖就捉弄你」好有你嘅口氣，讀落好有趣。

ENSpelling. teens, funny, candies, entrance. Copy each one three times and they will stay.

粵串字。teens、funny、candies、entrance。每個抄三次就記得。

ENReason sentences. The first reason I love Halloween is that I can play tricks on my friends. One verb, no because.

粵原因句。The first reason I love Halloween is that I can play tricks on my friends. 一個動詞，唔使 because。

ENOne wish for next year. You say Ocean Park did not happen this year. Close with a plan, and the ending will look forward instead of only looking back.

粵明年嘅願望。你話今年去唔到海洋公園。結尾加個計劃，文章就會望向前，唔止回顧過去。
