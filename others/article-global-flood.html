School magazine article — global flood myths

Task. Stories from Greek mythology, the Bible, ancient China and many other cultures describe gods or divine beings punishing people by flooding the world. In Greece, Zeus sank Atlantis. In the Bible, Noah built an ark. In China, the battle between Gong Gong and Nüwa brought devastating floods.

Write an article for your school magazine. Was there a real global flood in the past? Was it divine punishment? Give three pieces of evidence for your views.

Was there ever a global flood?

There are stories about a flood in almost every old culture. The Greeks talk about Zeus sinking Atlantis, the Bible talks about Noahs Ark and the Chinese have legends about a war between the gods Gong Gong and Nüwa that flooded the earth. Did a big flood really happen all over the world and was it because the gods were angry?

No. The history of the earth shows us that there was not one flood that covered the whole planet at the same time. It was not a punishment from God. The stories below are real stories of natural disasters from around the world that happened a long time ago. Here are 3 things that help us understand what really happened .

The earth underwent a major change some time ago when the last Ice Age came to an end. A meltwater pulse called 1B took place 11,500 years ago. This caused the great glaciers to collapse and the sea level to rise rapidly. It would have looked like the whole world flooding to the people living near the coast.

Second, when we look at buildings and things we see that there were big floods in the Middle East that might have inspired stories like Noahs Ark. Now we can see thick layers of mud from the rivers in a place called Mesopotamia which's now Iraq. This means that the floods of the Tigris and Euphrates rivers were very hard to predict. To the people living there it would have looked like all the world was flooding, because they did not know about the rest of the world.

Third, the ancient Chinese stories of floods match real events in the history of the earth. They speak of a goddess called Nüwa who repaired the sky after a flood, and of a later king called Emperor Yu who spent much time making the rivers less dangerous. Scientists have recently discovered there was a flood on the Yellow River in China about 1920 BCE. This big event is passed down from generation to generation. It turned into a legend about a war between the gods.”

But there was not one flood over the whole world as a punishment from the gods, and the old stories about floods are not just made-up stories. Finally, They are memories of terrifying natural disasters such as melting ice and overflowing rivers that changed the old world and left a mark on human history that we can still see today.
