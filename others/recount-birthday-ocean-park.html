What went well

Lively trip story with plenty for a reader to picture.

  • 🎂 Hook. Birthday + school trip + Ocean Park is an easy opening to follow.
  • 🎢 Action. Aquarium, Ocean Express, roller coasters, penguins, souvenirs — the day moves along.
  • 👥 Friends. Grouping with classmates and naming favourite rides feels real.
  • 😊 Ending. You close by saying you were happy and hope the school runs it again.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Past tense. Most of the trip uses went, liked, were. The opening can stay lively with it’s and I’m if you like that voice.
  • 💡 Articles. Add the before aquarium, souvenir shop, and scheme names where needed.
  • 💡 Word forms. exciting rides (not excited); elegant movement (not elegance).
  • 💡 Sentence joins. Fix comma splices and fragments after and after gathering…
  • 💡 Spelling. Sitting, check in, spend not expend.

My birthday trip to Ocean Park

On 6/7, it's my birthday and what's more is that my school said that on that day, who have joined holiday reading scheme can go to Ocean Park! And I'm one of the students who can go! Before that night, I was so excited that I even couldn't sleep at all.

On that day, I went there with my friends and after gathering all the students and teachers. We finally went inside to check-in and grouped together to start a trip in Ocean Park. When I was checking-in, I got several coupons to play, shopping and eat! Therefore I can save some money when I expended.

I got 2 friends to group with me. First we went to aquarium to admire the sea animals and what I like the most are sharks. Their movement when they were swimming were very elegance. Therefore I asked my groupmates to help me took some photos and selfies.

After that, we rode "Ocean train" to go to another side of Ocean Park. When we arrived, we ran as quickly as cheetahs to play different kinds of excited roller coasters. What I like the most are “The clowns roller coaster”, “Sating on a boat and get your body wet” and “360° spinning”. I screamed as loud as I could cause it really like your heart is pumping out!

When we felt hot, we went to “penguins” to cool down ourselves for a while then we went to souvenirs shop to buy some souvenirs. I bought an A4 file for my sister and a keychain for myself. Then we went to “Wong Chuk Hang” to eat McDonald’s because the food in Ocean Park are really expensive! And we rode back to Ocean Park to play for 2 hours then we back home.

This year, I spent my birthday very happy because I spent with my friends! I hope next year the school will also organise this activity for us!

Comments

The energy of the trip comes through well — readers can see the sharks, the rides, and the McDonald’s escape. Most fixes are past tense, articles, and a few word forms so the English matches a school recount.

ENClear timeline. Check-in, aquarium, Ocean Express, roller coasters, penguins, souvenirs, dinner, then home. Easy to follow.

粵時間線清楚。 入園、水族館、纜車、過山車、企鵝、紀念品、食飯、返屋企，跟得好順。

ENPersonal detail. Coupons, favourite rides, gifts for your sister and yourself — these make it your story, not a generic trip report.

粵個人細節。 優惠券、最鍾意機動遊戲、送妹妹同自己禮物，係你嘅故事。

ENPast tense in the main story. Once the trip starts, past verbs (went, liked, were) read more naturally. Your opening it’s my birthday and what’s more is that can stay for excitement.

粵主線用過去式。 行程開始後用 went、liked、were 順啲。開頭 it’s my birthday、what’s more is that 可以保留興奮感。

ENWatch sentence joins. After I went with my friends, start a new sentence for After everyone had gathered instead of joining with and after gathering.

粵留意造句。 I went with my friends 之後開新句寫 After everyone had gathered，唔好用 and after gathering 硬接。

ENEnding works. Saying you hope the school organises it again next year rounds off the piece nicely.

粵結尾 OK。 希望下年學校再辦，收得好自然。
