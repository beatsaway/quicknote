Persuasive speech about Chinese white dolphins

You are giving a persuasive speech at a school assembly about Chinese white dolphins, the pink dolphins that live around Lantau Island. Explain what they are like, why they are endangered, and what your audience can do. End with a strong call to action.

What went well

You sound like someone who really likes these dolphins, and that warmth carries the whole speech.

  • 🎯 Structure. Greeting and name, facts, two threats, two solutions, and a call to action. That is the full persuasive shape.
  • 📊 Evidence. About 40 dolphins and a 70% drop turn a big idea into a number the audience can feel.
  • ❤️ Voice. “Cute and friendly” and “Act now” speak straight to your schoolmates.
  • 🐬 Knowledge. Top predator and food chain show you understand why this species matters.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Sentences. A few fragments and run-ons need splitting, for example “The first threat that they are facing which is overfishing.” One idea per sentence is easier to say aloud.
  • 💡 Word forms. habitat, bodies, numbers, fish and squid. Use prevent something, not prevent something happen.
  • 💡 Facts. The 40 dolphins are the Hong Kong population, not the world population. Say in Hong Kong waters.
  • 💡 Persuasion. Add one more direct “you” line early, for example “Every plastic bottle you drop can end up in their home.”

Speech to the school (draft)

Good morning, Principal, teachers and fellow students. I’m Michelle Luk. Today I want to talk about Chinese white dolphins.

The characteristic of Chinese white dolphins are friendly, they love to play with us. Their body are in pink colour and they usually feed on fishes and squids. Their hokitats are in the seas around Lantau Island. They are so important because they are the top predator in the local food chain.

In these recent years, Chinese white dolphins are listed in the endangered animals. species. There are only around 40 dolphins in the world which is 70% less compared with 10 years ago.

The first threat that they are facing which is overfishing. The fishers always werfishing around those areas which the dolphins can't have enough food for them to eat and survive. Then they will starve to death so their number decreased rapidly.

The second threat is water pollution People throw the rubbish, like plastic bottles tissues and leftover food into the sea. When the dolphins eat them, they bodies will sick and toxic so increase the death.

To prevent this situation happen, we should throw the rubbish into rubbish bins but not into the sea. And we can also buy Ash in other countries or less fish so It can prevent overfishing and water pollution happen.

If you don't want the cute and friendly Chinese White dolphins extinct. Act now before it's too late!. Thank you!
