Opinion writing. The school rules

Topic. The school rules. What do you think of the school rules? Are they strict or lenient?

Give your view. Use examples of rules you like and rules you find too strict.

  • Success criteria. Clear stance. Examples. A short conclusion.
  • Only short yellow phrases are suggested edits. The rest is the draft as written.
  • Click flow. Yellow to green plus comment. Only one comment at a time.
Useful words strict, lenient, appearance, eyebrows, fringe, prefect, phone, lunchtime, face to face, come back, Although, First of all, Secondly, What I like most, In conclusion

What went well

Balanced view. One strict rule, one phone rule, and one rule you like.

  • 💇 Hair detail. Fringe and eyebrows makes the appearance rule feel real.
  • 📱 Phone example. Coupons vs lunch chat shows you understand the school’s worry.
  • 🍜 Lunch out. Form 1 going out to eat is a clear plus.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Spelling. appearance, eyebrows, disciplined, always.
  • 💡 Agreement. Are they (rules). doesn’t have. won’t happen.
  • 💡 Linkers. First of all / Secondly / What I like most / In conclusion.
  • 💡 Although. Do not use Although … but in the same sentence.

The school rules

What do you think of the school rules? Is it strict or lenient? For me, I think it's quite strict sometimes, especially appearence! However, I think there are also some rules quite good.

For me, I think the appetirence rules are really strict! For example, the fringe cannot over the eyebrowns, which our disclipired teachers and prefect alway- catch me! I really hate it! I think if it won't disturb others, then why they should catch us?

The second one is that we can't use phone during lunch time. We can only use it during using coupons to buy lunch. Our school said that they scared we will lose the face-to-face relationships. However I think it won't happened because we also want to play and chat with friends during lunch time.

What I like the most is that we can go out to eat lunch outside in Form. !! It's because our class teacher said that our school don't have any shopping malls nearby so they trust us won't forget to back to school.

Although I know school rules are mostly strict but I know the school also want us to be good students and be plain but I hope they can strict rules to become less strict in some days.
