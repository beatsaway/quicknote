Opinion — cancel the school trip?

Your school planned an exchange trip to a country with an Ebola outbreak. Parents and teachers are arguing about whether to cancel it.

Write a short opinion piece. Say whether you agree or disagree with cancelling the trip. Give two reasons for your view. Start with one clear sentence that states your opinion.

  • Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits; the rest is the student’s draft as written.
  • Success criteria. Clear agree/disagree in the opening. Two reasons linked to safety or learning. Short conclusion.

Draft length (student), about 95 words. Task length is fine for a short opinion piece.

Useful words (from the task) (English · 粵語) Ebola(伊波拉), outbreak(疫情爆發), exchange trip(交流團), cancel(取消), agree / disagree(同意／不同意), safety(安全), risk(風險), public health(公共衛生), parents(家長), postpone(延期)

What went well

You took a clear side and kept the focus on students.

  • 👍 Clear opening. Your first sentence states you agree with cancelling. That meets the task.
  • 👍 Safety reason. You explain that student safety should come before the trip.
  • 👍 Learning reason. You argue the trip would not be a good learning experience with health worries and rules.
  • 👍 Sensible balance. You admit missing the exchange is sad, but safety comes first.
  • 👍 Topic words. You use outbreak, exchange, and cancel naturally.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Signposting. Try Firstly and Secondly so your two reasons are easy to spot.
  • 💡 Spelling. Use it is safe, not its safe (possessive its vs contraction it's / it is).
  • 💡 Word choice. Cut the extra line It's not safe, for them after you already said the trip is a bad idea.
  • 💡 Task vocabulary. Work in risk, public health, parents, or postpone where they fit.
  • 💡 Ending. Replace The school made the choice with a stronger line such as Postponing the trip was the right decision.

I completely agree with cancelling the school exchange trip because of the Ebola outbreak.

The safety of students is the priority for any school. Taking teenagers to a place with an outbreak is not a good idea. It's not safe, for them.

Missing out on exchange is sad but student safety comes first. The school should wait until its safe to travel to that region.

The trip is meant to be a learning experience. With all the worry and medical rules it wouldn't be a good experience. The school made the choice.

Comments

You already did the hard part. You picked a side, gave two sensible reasons, and did not wander off the topic. The yellow bits are small polish fixes, not a full rewrite.

For a stronger opinion piece, label reason 1 (safety / risk) and reason 2 (poor learning experience). Mention parents or public health once if you can. End with a clear verdict, not a flat sentence like The school made the choice.
