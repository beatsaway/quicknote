The sports day

We had sports day on the 13/10 & 14/10 in Hammer Hill Sports Ground. This year. I felt extremely exhausted and unforgettable!

On the first day, I join Houses 4x400m relay, the 200m first heat and ting jump. I felt very excited for the sports day at first, however after I tried the try jump in long jump, then a teacher called me to run Houses 4x400m relay. Therefore I went and after the relay, I immediately went back there for the first and second jump. My friend pulled my hand to go. because I was so tired after 4x400m relay. Unfortunately, when we went there, the teacher said it was finish and he used my try jump result to be the final record. Surprisingly, my try jump result is 3.00m which I got the 2nd runner up. At that moment, my tears were started to fall my eyes. Although I got 2nd runner up but I can do the first and second jump, I'm pretty sure I could get the champion because the champion result is 3.3m and when really I practise my long jump, my result was nearly 4.0m. I was heart broken. Next year, I won't go to join Houses 4x400m relay again!

The second day. I went to 200m final heat, classes 4x100m relay and Houses 4x100m and 400m. That day it was the most exhausting day ever! When I was running the 200m and 400m, I heard a lot of cheering. Sound from my friends and Houses. After I ran 200m & 400m, I cried again. My friends came to comfort me because after I ran 400m, I can't move and stand up.

This year I think the sports day is very fruitful and unforgettable Next year, I will practise more before the sports day to prevent my leg become shot!
