What makes a good teacher?

A good teacher goes beyond entertainment—they challenge, believe in, and make a lasting impact on students. Three essential qualities define such teachers:

*1. Keen Observation:* Great teachers pay close attention, scanning the classroom for students who need support. They recognize those who struggle in silence or finish early, ensuring that no student fades into the background. Every student feels seen and valued.

*2. Empathetic Encouragement:* They show care and support, especially in challenging situations. Rather than criticize, inspiring teachers respond patiently, breaking down concepts until students understand. They create an environment where mistakes are viewed as opportunities for growth, allowing students to learn without fear.

*3. Adaptability:* Understanding that each student learns differently, great teachers adjust their methods to fit individual styles. They employ various teaching techniques—lectures, group work, and hands-on activities—while monitoring student responses to maintain engagement and facilitate learning.

Such teachers, like my primary school English teacher Mrs. Lin, instill courage and belief in their students.
