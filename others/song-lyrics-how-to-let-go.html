About this piece

Original song lyrics — English check

A friend is writing an original English song and wants the lyrics to sound natural and correct. This is not a DSE article or story task. Short lines, repetition and emotion are normal in songs.

  • Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits; the rest is the draft as written.
  • Click flow. Yellow → green + comment. Only one comment at a time. Click the comment box, elsewhere, or green again to hide it. Click green with comment open to go back to yellow.
  • Edits stay small. Keep the songwriter’s words and How to… pattern where possible.
  • Repeating Love connects us all in the chorus is fine for a song. No change suggested there.

What works well

The song already has a clear emotional arc and a strong hook.

  • 👍 Theme. Letting go, failure, loneliness, then love as a guide. Easy to follow.
  • 👍 Parallel hooks. How to let go?, How to go on, How to move on tie the verses together.
  • 👍 Chorus. Simple, memorable line. Repetition fits pop lyrics.
  • 👍 Bridge. Kind shift from pain to life’s a long game and you’re loved.

Could be even better

  • 💡 How to… lines. These are hook phrases, not full questions. Add missing words only, e.g. the pain. No question mark on How to relieve the pain.
  • 💡 Fix small grammar slips. answers, all the time, Shut down, Kindness. It’s up to you, true not truth.
  • 💡 Light touch elsewhere. Only change words that sound wrong to a listener. Keep lines like We are the one if you like them.

Comments

The feeling of the song is already strong. The yellow bits are mostly grammar and phrasing so an English listener hears it clearly when sung.

ENClear mood. The lyrics move from confusion and failure to love and connection. A listener can follow the story even in short lines.

粵情緒清楚。 由迷惘、失敗去到愛同連繫，短句都跟到條故事線。

ENGood hook lines. How to let go?, How to go on and How to move on work well as verse openers. They sound like a real song structure.

粵開句有力。 How to let go?、How to go on、How to move on 做每段開首好似歌詞結構。

ENChorus fits the genre. Love connects us all is simple and easy to sing. Repeating it eight times is normal in pop lyrics, not a mistake.

粵副歌合格式。 Love connects us all 简单易唱，重複八次喺流行歌好正常，唔係錯。

ENHow to… is not a full question. How to relieve the pain is a hook line, like a title. It does not need a question mark. A real question would be How do you relieve the pain? but you can keep your How to… pattern instead.

粵How to… 唔係完整問句。 How to relieve the pain 係 hook／標題式句子，唔使加問號。真正問句係 How do you relieve the pain?，但你可保留 How to… 寫法。

ENSmall grammar fixes. Watch all the answers, all the time, Shut down, Kindness. It’s up to you and true not truth. Small changes only.

粵小文法位。 留意 all the answers、all the time、Shut down、Kindness. It’s up to you、true 唔係 truth。只改必要位。

ENYour line, your choice. We are the one, Winking sleepless eyes and Guide you to go can stay if they fit your melody and meaning.

粵你嘅字，你決定。 We are the one、Winking sleepless eyes、Guide you to go 如果啱旋律同意思，可以保留。
