Task. Write an article for your school magazine.

In a wartime situation in Hong Kong, do you think drones are more useful or less useful than robot dogs for your own survival? Give three reasons.

What went well

Clear pick: robot dog > drone, plus intro + three reasons + close.

  • 🐕 Stance. You choose a robot dog early — markers can see your side.
  • 🏙️ Companion idea. Loneliness / animal companion fits “your own survival,” not only weapons.
  • 🔋 Durability hint. Saying a drone breaks more easily is a useful comparison — keep it, but make the logic clearer.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Intro context. Open with wartime Hong Kong (task setting), not a vague “speaking of technology” hook.
  • 💡 Task words. Say clearly that drones are less useful than robot dogs (or robot dogs are more useful).
  • 💡 Linkers. Prefer First of all / Secondly / Last but not least.
  • 💡 Reason 2 logic. “Fight … flexibility” is confusing — compare terrain / battery / noise instead.
  • 💡 School tone + close. Soften gun talk; fix In a whole → In conclusion and restate your view.

Drones or robot dogs for survival in Hong Kong?

Speaking of survival with technology, I think lots of people would think about robots. However, if you really need one, would you rather choose a drone or a robot dog? I would want to have a robot dog.

My first reason is that you can keep it as a pet, so you won’t get bored when you’re lonely. It can also help you by equipping a gun, so you will be safe.

Secondly, it is easier for a drone to break than a robot dog. That means if you had a fight with a person having a drone, you would win easily because of its flexibility.

Lastly, it can make decisions for you, like what you need to do during times when you have no food. An animal companion is always beside you when you need it. A drone just flies with you, only protecting you with weapons.

In a whole, you can choose anything after reading my work though. It's your own opinion after all.
