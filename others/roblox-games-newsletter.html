Task

Roblox games to play with friends

To have fun socialising while not being in school this Easter, many students want to stay in touch with their friends on Roblox. Write an article to recommend at least 2 such games for your school newsletter.

Give at least 3 reasons for each. You could mention the gameplay, visuals, sounds, characters, scenes, actions, items, tasks, special events, etc.

Useful words recommend, newsletter, hang out, socialise, gameplay, vivid, character, scene, sound effect, task, special event, multiplayer, co-op, avatar, reason, audience, holiday

What went well

  • Two games named: Adopt Me! and Brookhaven RP.
  • Each game has three clear parts with headings — matches “at least 3 reasons”.
  • Friendly tone and a clear start and end; fits hanging out in the holidays.
  • Mentions tasks, events, building together — good link to the task wording.

Could be even better

  • Add a sentence or two about what it looks or sounds like (colours, music, sound effects) — the task invited that.
  • Make it sound more like a newsletter (e.g. “Hi everyone,” or a one-line hello to readers).
  • One line mixes you and we — pick one and stick to it.

Pupil’s writing

Easter is here! School is out, and this is the perfect time to stay connected with your friends. One of the best ways to do this is Roblox. Roblox is an amazing platform to virtually hang out with your friends. Here are two top Roblox games that you can play with your friends this Easter:

Adopt Me!

Shared Goals: Play together and help each other in raising cute pets, earning Bucks by completing tasks, and exploring this colorful world. Playing together is super fun!

Creative Hangout: Build and decorate your dream home together. You can let your imagination run wild and create your own space to virtually hang out together!

Mini Games and Events: Play various mini-games like Egg Hunt and enjoy events. These mini-games and events are constantly updated with new and exciting content.

Brookhaven RP

Endless Roleplay: You can make up any story you want in this huge open world! You can be neighbors, detectives, or explorers with your friends. The options are endless.

In Interactive World, you can drive cool cars, fly helicopters, cook in houses, or visit different places like the police station or hospital. We can always find something to do together.

Collaborative Building: Work together to build and furnish your own house plot in Brookhaven. This will help you be creative and work together with others.

Get your friends together and play Adopt Me! or Brookhaven RP to make some great memories over Easter break!

For reference

Hello everyone, During the Easter holiday lots of us want to socialise even when we are not at school. My friends and I use Roblox to hang out online, and I would like to recommend two games that work really well in multiplayer. The first one is Adopt Me! You can team up to care for pets and complete daily tasks to earn Bucks — the co-op gameplay keeps everyone involved. I also like how vivid the map looks: bright buildings, cute animals, and holiday scenes when there is a special event. A third reason to try it is the housing part — you and your friends can decorate rooms together and show off items you collected, which feels creative and relaxing. Another game I enjoy is Brookhaven RP. First, it is great for roleplay because you choose your own story and avatar, and every session can be different. Second, the town is huge: you can drive around, visit places like the café or hospital, and there are little sound effects that make each scene feel alive. Third, you can share a house plot and furnish it as a group, so you practise planning and helping each other. If you are bored over the break, invite a few classmates and try these two — they are easy to learn and good for staying in touch. Happy Easter! Chris Wong
