Proposal — Atlantis research (Spain)

Task. Spain has a long coastline and a strong interest in history and archaeology. Some researchers believe Atlantis may lie beneath the Atlantic, near the Canary Islands or elsewhere off the Spanish coast. The government is deciding whether to fund teams to explore and search for it.

Write a proposal to the Spanish government. Mention three reasons why funding Atlantis research would benefit Spain.

  • Success criteria: three reasons with an example each; linkers First of all / Second / Last but not least; end with In conclusion or To summarise.
  • Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits. The draft stops at Lastly — click that highlight for a model third reason and conclusion.
  • Click flow. Yellow → green + comment. Only one comment at a time. Click the comment box, elsewhere, or green again to hide it. Click green with comment open to go back to yellow.
Useful words proposal, fund, research team, expedition, Atlantis, archaeology, tourism, discovery, reputation, economy, evidence, for example, First of all, Last but not least

What went well

Clear stance and proposal structure started well.

  • 📌 Opening stance. You open by saying funding Atlantis research is a great idea.
  • 1️⃣ First of all. You use the required linker and link interest to honour for Spain and the team.
  • 2️⃣ Secondly. You try a second reason about public attention and more funding.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Finish the task. Add a full Last but not least reason (with for example) and a short conclusion.
  • 💡 Address Spain. Write to the Spanish government — use Spain, not your country.
  • 💡 One example per reason. Success criteria ask for an example each time.
  • 💡 Stronger benefits. Soft ideas like “feel happier” are weaker than tourism, reputation, or economy.
  • 💡 Collocation. Prefer fund research into Atlantis / fund an expedition, not fund for researching.

Proposal: fund Atlantis research in Spain

In my opinion, I think it is a great idea to fund for researching Atlantis as it can benefit your country for a lot of reasons.

First of all, it can get people's interest, so if they found any evidence about Atlantis, you and the searching teams will be benefited for an honor with a special name for finding them.

Secondly, people will pay more attention to searching hints from Atlantis, so they want to know more about it. In this time, if you fund more to the searching team, it will make people feel happier.

Lastly,(unfinished)
