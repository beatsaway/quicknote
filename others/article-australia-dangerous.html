School magazine article — Australia

Task. Some people think Australia is dangerous because of wildlife and natural disasters. Others say it is a safe developed country to live in.

Write an article for your school magazine. Do you agree or disagree that Australia is a dangerous place to live? Give three reasons.

  • Success criteria: clear agree or disagree; introduction; three reasons; conclusion.
  • Only short yellow phrases are suggested edits; the rest is the draft as written.
  • Click flow. Yellow → green + comment. Only one comment at a time. Click the comment box, elsewhere, or green again to hide it. Click green with comment open to go back to yellow.
Useful words venomous, statistics, coastal, antivenom, habitat, reputation, developed country, medical care, social welfare, modern infrastructure, extreme heatwave, sense of community, crime rate, bushfire, caution, risk, quality of life, rare, agree, disagree, for example, First of all, Last but not least

What went well

Clear disagree + intro, three reasons, and a closing question.

  • ❌ Disagree. “I absolutely do not agree” is easy for markers to tick.
  • 🕷️ Reputation vs risk. You challenge the venomous-spider image with a statistics-style claim.
  • 🦘 Real risk. Road kangaroos at night feels more practical than movie myths.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Linkers. Use First of all / Second / Last but not least instead of Number one / two / three.
  • 💡 For example. Add one concrete example under each reason.
  • 💡 Conclusion label. Start the last paragraph with In conclusion or To summarise.
  • 💡 Word bank. Weave in statistics, habitat, coastal, reputation, or rare.
  • 💡 Typos. Fix incomplete phrases (in the might, the e drivers, no hunt).

Is Australia a dangerous place to live?

Many netizens say that Australia is a dangerous place to live, and I absolutely do not agree with that.

Number one, people are aware that the venomous creatures in the might kill them. But researchers have found that most of the people killed by animals are widely killed by kangaroos, and different mammals, and zero case has been linked up to a venomous spider.

Number two, many people in Australia live near the coastal areas, where the sharks and crocodiles are. But they are easy to avoid, since the city areas are near the coast it will make more sense for the government to pay more attention. Since the city is near the coastal areas the natural habitats live in the middle of the country, not near the people at all.😃

Number three, most of the creatures there are naturally shy, they do their own thing. They don’t bother the people. So it made sense for the people to no hunt them since they don’t disturb them. The creature they should be looking out for is the kangaroo, it's not cause of the au boxing kangaroos. But they cross the street when it’s night time and the e drivers don’t notice them.

So is Australia really a dangerous place to live in? I don't think so
