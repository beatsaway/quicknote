The Myth of Innocence: Switzerland’s WWII Neutrality Was an Illusion

We think of Switzerland as a country of mountains, good chocolate and a very strong sense of neutrality. Switzerland was a country that was surrounded by powerful countries during World War II but did not take sides. I don't think Switzerland was really neutral when I look at what really happened. It was not invaded. It dealt with other countries economically and politically in ways that demonstrate neutrality is more than simply not going to war. It's about doing the right thing.”

Three things that prove this

Dealing with the Enemy: First Switzerlands economy relied on trade with Nazi Germany that helped the war effort. Swiss factories made goods, weapons and machines for Germany to avoid being invaded. They also helped Germany and traded with them. This kept the German economy alive even when other countries refused to do business with them.

Secondly, the Swiss National Bank took a lot of gold from Nazi Germany. Some of the gold was taken from people in the concentration camps, including wedding rings, gold teeth, and other valuables. The bank also confiscated money stolen from banks in European countries. By accepting this gold, Switzerland profited from the events of the Holocaust.

Not Helping Refugees Switzerland also didn't help refugees trying to escape from the Nazis. Some were allowed to stay, but the country wouldn’t let most Jewish refugees in, even when they were running from death. The border guards would often turn families away and send them back to places where they would be killed.

To conclude, to say that Switzerland had no choice because it is a small country does not take into account the picture of what is right and wrong. Neutrality is not the absence of war. It’s about not helping a country that’s doing bad stuff and not making money from bad things. Switzerland might not have been invaded, It helped Nazi Germany by trading with them, taking their stolen gold, and not helping people who needed it. This implies that Switzerlands neutrality was not true.
