Was there a great flood in human history?

Many old stories describe a terrible flood. Some say the water was as high as a mountain and almost covered the whole world. I do not think that kind of flood ever happened. Huge local floods did happen, and they help explain the myths. They do not prove that one flood covered Earth.

First of all, the Chinese story of Yu is about a serious flood in one land, not the whole planet. Yu worked for years to divert floodwaters so people could farm again. Later his son became king of the Xia dynasty. This reads like a hero story about a river. It does not show that water stood over every mountain on Earth.

Another example comes from ancient Sumer. In that myth, the goddess Mami created humans because the Igigi gods did not want to work for the Anunnaki. Before the flood, the god Enki warned some humans. This is a religious story from one region, Mesopotamia. Gods creating people and then sending a flood is not the same as a historical report.

These two examples should not be treated as proof. They were passed down by word of mouth, so details can grow bigger over time. They also disagree. Yu saves people by digging channels. The Sumerian story is about gods punishing humans. If both described the same worldwide event, the basic facts would match. Many cultures have flood stories because many places have rivers and heavy rain, not because one wave covered every land.

There are stronger reasons to reject a flood that covered the entire world. There is not enough water on Earth to cover the highest mountains. A flood that high would also have killed almost everyone at once, but people in different places kept living and building. A real worldwide flood would leave the same clear signs everywhere. What we find instead are local floods, such as rivers bursting their banks.

That is why I claim there was no huge flood on Earth that covered the entire world. The myths are worth reading. They are not enough to prove a mountain-high flood happened.
