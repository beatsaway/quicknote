Article — keeping patient records private

Hospital records contain private health information. When medical data is not kept secure, serious harm can follow.

Write a short article (100–130 words). Give at least two examples of what could go wrong when medical data is leaked. Give two ideas for how hospitals or the government could protect medical data better.

  • Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits; the rest is the student’s draft as written.
  • Success criteria. At least two examples of harm. Two ideas for hospitals and/or government.

Draft length (student), about 155 words. Trim to 100–130 words by cutting the off-topic line and tightening each example.

Useful words (from the task) (English · 粵語) medical data(醫療數據), patient records(病人紀錄), leak(外洩), embarrassment(尷尬), discrimination(歧視), blackmail(勒索), lose trust(失去信任), stricter access rules(更嚴格查閱規定), staff training(員工培訓), encryption(加密)

Could be even better

  • 💡 Word limit. Cut about 25 words. Delete I think the only solution is to work harder then. Keep one clear sentence per example.
  • 💡 Grammar. Use leaked and seen by others, not getting leaked out and make other people see it.
  • 💡 Two different harms. Harm 1 is health privacy. Harm 2 is ID theft by criminals. Do not say the doctor hacks the account.
  • 💡 Hospital line. Rephrase anti-scamming apps in two short sentences with encryption, stricter access rules and staff who leak records.
  • 💡 Develop harm. Say why a leak hurts, e.g. embarrassment at school, discrimination at work, or criminals using health facts to blackmail someone.

Lots of people use e-hospital records now as it is faster and easier to use. However, some doctors would access data without getting the patient's authorization. It always makes a lot of trouble.

At first, the patient wouldn't want their medical data getting leaked out and make other people see it. Even if it's just normal diseases, they may not want anything (like their name) to be seen.

Next, the patient's account might be hacked by the doctor even though there's just a little information about the patient. After a while, his/her money will be taken. It is also very hard to get the money back. I think the only solution is to work harder then.

Hospitals can create some anti-scamming apps to prevent doctors from hacking into a patient's account. The government can call the citizens to focus while seeing a doctor. That means if they did anything that felt suspicious, they should be aware of the doctor.
