Dear Martin,

I feel that you’re a bit down in these because of friends relationship, handling a ton of homework and your appearance. Let me give some suggestions.

First of all, I know you have argument with your friends recently. To feel lonely, why don't you apologize to your friend first? When you're apologizing, show your sincere heart that makes your friends feel that you want to rebuild the relation so that they may forgive you and you won't feel lonely. As a result, you can hang out with them well.

Besides, I understand that you can't get rid of your homework, quizzes and study for your last test. Here are some tips help you to get rid of your problems. You plan your schedule by writting timetable everyday. Therefore you can have a regular routine and sleep earlier for the next day. This way, you can prioritise well by having regular timetable.

Last but not least, I feel that you’re worried about your appearance right now. Actually, I also used to have this experience before. Luckily, I seek help from my parents and they buy some skin care products to me. My skin have been better since then. I suggest you can ask your parents to buy skin products for you. To help you lose weight, I suggest you can do more exercise regularly. You can do some simple exercises, for example, walking for 20 minutes, biking and doing yoga. Therefore you can lose weight faster and free your stress.

Martin, if you want I give some suggestions, I hope these suggestions can help you get rid of your stress. remember you're not alone. I'm always free! Looking forwards for your reply!

Best wishes,
Chris

Dear Martin,

I was sorry to hear you have been feeling so low. Friendship worries, school pressure, and doubts about your appearance are heavy, but they can get better. Here are a few ideas.

If you argued with friends, talk calmly when you are ready. A sincere apology can help rebuild the relationship, but listening matters too. You do not have to face loneliness alone.

For homework and tests, write a simple timetable each day so you can prioritise tasks and sleep earlier. Short study blocks with breaks often work better than panicking the night before.

About appearance, I once felt the same. My parents helped me choose basic skincare, and gentle exercise such as walking or yoga relieved my stress. Health and confidence grow slowly. You are more than how you look on one day.

Remember, you are not alone. Message me anytime. I look forward to your reply.

Best wishes,
Chris
