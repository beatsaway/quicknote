Topic. Our school picnic day

Write about what happened on picnic day, what you enjoyed most, and how you felt.

Useful words picnic, Clear Water Bay, country park, spectacular, barbecue, marshmallow, explore, shuttle bus, truth or dare, treat someone to a meal, fruitful, unforgettable

What went well

A whole day, in order, with one story at the end that no one else could tell.

  • 🎯 Clear timeline. Arrival, food, walk, game, photos, bus, ball games. The reader never asks what happened next.
  • 😂 Funny bet. Two balls, a free meal, and a friend with no way out. That is the best moment in the piece.
  • 🌊 Sense of place. The 30-minute walk around the country park makes the place feel big.
  • 💓 Feelings that move. Scared before handball, pleased after. The day feels lived, not listed.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Past tense. caught, not catch. could with would, not can.
  • 💡 Word forms. picnic (not pionic), hard (not harsh), treat me to a meal.
  • 💡 One idea per sentence. The Meanwhile sentence carries a teacher, a graduate, other classes and a game.
  • 💡 Join and split. news. about is one phrase cut in two. Time flies, it was is two sentences joined by a comma.
  • 💡 Spelling. Clear Water Bay takes a capital C, and barbecue is the full word for bbq.

Comments

A full day in the right order, and the handball bet gives it a proper story. Most fixes are tense, word forms and two sentences that were split or run together.

ENClear order. You move from arrival to food to walking to the game to the bus to the ball games, so the day is easy to follow.

粵次序清楚。由去到、食嘢、行山、玩遊戲、搭車返學到打球，一日嘅流程好易跟。

ENThe bet is your best paragraph. Two balls against one free meal gives the ending tension, and the last line pays it off.

粵打賭一段最精彩。接兩個波換一餐，令結尾有張力，最後仲要找數。

ENFeelings change. Scared before handball, pleased after. That movement is what makes a recount feel real.

粵心情有變化。打波前驚，打完開心。呢種轉變令記敘文真實。
