Global warming

Global warming is the increase in Earths temperature because of actions since the industrial revolution. The burning of fuels like coal, oil and natural gas adds a lot of greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide and methane into the air. These gases trap heat near Earth working like a blanket that stops the heat from escaping the planet.

Global warming is already having its impact over the world. The polar ice sheets and mountain glaciers are melting faster which causes sea levels to rise and puts lying towns in danger. Heatwaves, droughts, big wildfires and strong storms are happening often. Causing more destruction. Global warming also affects animals changes the chemistry of the oceans and puts food and water at risk for everyone.

Fixing this problem requires an effort. Switching from fuels to clean energy sources like solar and wind protecting forests and farming in ways that do not harm the Earth are steps. Fighting warming is not a good idea; it is a necessary action to keep the planet safe, for our children and for future generations.
