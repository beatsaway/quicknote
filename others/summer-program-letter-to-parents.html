Kari asks Mom and Dad to support her application to a selective two-week summer academic program overseas. Yellow highlights suggest clearer English or a slightly more formal tone—her ideas stay the same.

Vocabulary hints (English · 粵語) application(申請), selective(挑選嚴格), support(支持), mock exams(模擬試), revision timetable(溫習時間表), budget(預算), supervise(看管), safeguarding(保障安全), sign-off(信末署名)

What went well

Clear purpose and you answer worries before they grow.

  • 👍 Introduction — readers know you want help with an application.
  • 👍 Benefits — you link the program to university applications and independence.
  • 👍 Concerns — you name mock exams, cost, and safety.
  • 👍 Next step — you suggest talking at the weekend.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Paragraphs: split the long middle paragraph so mock exams, money, and safety each get a short section.
  • 💡 Tone: Dear Mom and Dad, often fits school formal letters better than Hi.
  • 💡 Cost: focus on savings and planning the budget with your parents; skip a part-time job promise unless your teacher asks for it.
  • 💡 Safety: name staff / teachers on campus instead of only “I promise it is safe.”
  • 💡 Ending: put With love, / Best wishes, on the line above your name, not Kari Wong loves you.

Letter to Mom and Dad (summer program)

Hi Mom and Dad,

I am writing to ask for your help with my application to a selective two-week summer academic program at a top university in another country. This chance could really improve my college applications by showing that I am independent and dedicated to my studies.

I understand your worries about my mock exams, costs, and safety.

I have made a realistic revision schedule, plans to help pay for things through savings and a part-time job, and I promise that the program will be safe with the right supervision.

Thank you for your help. I would like to talk about the details more this weekend. Thanks for helping me.

Kari Wong loves you.

Comments
