School magazine — China’s Great Firewall

China uses a system called the Great Firewall to block many foreign websites and control what people can see and say online. Some people criticise it, but others say it has benefits for the country.

Write an article for your school magazine (about 200 words). Explain what the Great Firewall is. Mention three reasons why some people think it is a good thing. Include an introduction and a conclusion.

  • Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits; the rest is the student’s draft as written.
  • Success criteria. Brief explanation of the Great Firewall. Introduction and conclusion. Three reasons why it could be seen as a good thing.

Draft length (student), about 175 words.

Useful words (English · 粵語) Great Firewall(防火長城), firewall(防火牆), censor(審查), block(封鎖), control(管控), harmful content(有害內容), surveillance(監控), stability(穩定), protect(保護), foreign websites(外國網站), national security(國家安全), misinformation(錯誤資訊), culture(文化), online safety(網絡安全), government(政府)

Why some people support the Great Firewall

The Great Firewall is a wall-like system that prevents Chinese people from using social media from other countries, like Instagram, Facebook and even Youtube. I will tell you three of my opinions about it.

At first, it could secure data on public Wi-Fi, that means the people there will only use the Internet from China. I think it is quite secure from preventing any bad things happening in there.

Secondly, there might be misinformation in other countries that will make the Chinese feel stressed and make things become serious. Just because of the Great Firewall, those things won't happen easily.

Lastly, if you really want to get into other social media apps, be cautious, as the government created a complicated 'list' before letting people in social media apps from other countries. The most famous one is VPN. You need a lot of personal information and a whole setting of instructions to get in. In addition, you have to pay for it.

In a whole, I think there a lot of advantages in the Great Firewall. I hope more countries can make a setting like this!
