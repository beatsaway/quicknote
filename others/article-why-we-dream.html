Every night our brains take us on an adventure. What are dreams for? Our science mag explores what we think about in our dreams. REM sleep is when our brains are in a state of high activity and this is when most of our dreams happen. Scientists still don’t know why we dream. They have some good thoughts.

Why do we dream? Here are three reasons:

  • Working Through Memories. One more theory is that dreams help us work through the events of the day. Our brain is a computer that cleans up all of the information that we got during the day and keeps only what is important.
  • Emotional Therapy. Dreams also help us with emotions. When we dream about something stressful or exciting, our brain can think about it without getting too upset.
  • The Visual Cortex Defended. The theory is that dreams may help us to see better. In the dark, our brains can start to depend on senses like hearing and forget about sight. While we sleep, the part of our brain that helps us to see is still active.

Dreams are not strange stories made up by our brain. Dreams are important for our brains, whether we are remembering things, feeling better, or keeping our brains working. Think of all the cool things your brain does when you fall asleep with your eyes closed.
