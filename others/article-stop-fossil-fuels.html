School magazine article — fossil fuels

Task. Fossil fuels such as oil, coal and natural gas were formed from ancient plants and animals under heat and pressure. They power modern life, but burning them adds carbon dioxide to the atmosphere. (Stimulus video: Venus warning.)

Write an article for your school magazine. Do you agree or disagree that we should stop using fossil fuels? Give three reasons.

  • Success criteria: clear agree or disagree; three reasons with an example each; end with In conclusion or To summarise.
  • Only short yellow phrases are suggested edits. The draft has only two reasons — click the yellow before the ending for a model third reason + conclusion label.
  • Click flow. Yellow → green + comment. Only one comment at a time. Click the comment box, elsewhere, or green again to hide it. Click green with comment open to go back to yellow.
Useful words fossil fuel, petroleum, natural gas, coal, renewable energy, emission, climate, economy, agree, disagree, for example, First of all, Last but not least

What went well

Clear agree stance and a vivid “loop” idea.

  • ✅ Agree. You state your view in the first sentence.
  • 🌡️ Climate. Carbon dioxide trapping heat is on topic.
  • 🏥 Health loop. Pollution → sickness → more building → more gases is creative.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Three reasons. You promise three but only write two. Add Last but not least.
  • 💡 For example. Put one concrete example under each reason.
  • 💡 Linkers. Prefer First of all; end with In conclusion (not In a whole).
  • 💡 Word bank. Decomposable fits rubbish / packaging. For energy, use renewable energy.
  • 💡 Agreement. burning fossil fuels makes; get even worse (not more worse).

Should we stop using fossil fuels?

In my opinion, I agree that we should stop using fossil fuels as it will harm us from different categories. I will tell you three of the reasons why I think it is harmful.

First, and the most important reason is it will harm the environment a lot, as the fossil fuels that are burnt makes lots of carbon dioxide. They trap heat and make the Earth hotter.

Secondly, it pollutes the air with different kinds of harmful gases, so more people will get sick. Hospitals would be full and builders need to build more of them. Building will make more harmful gases and it will become a loop. That means it will just get more worse.

In a whole, I recommend to use things that are decomposable to relieve this situation. Let’s start to protect our world!
