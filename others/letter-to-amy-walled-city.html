Letter to Amy — convince her not to go

Amy,

I would advise you to stay away from Kowloon Walled City during the 1980s despite the fact that it looks like an adventurous location. This was a very dangerous and risky location. By interviewing the locals, you would get labeled as a spy; therefore, you would become a threat to the triads.

Going to unlicensed dentists might result in the development of certain diseases. Interacting with the food could get you sick because there were numerous diseases in the area.

Your friend, Kari.
