The experience of playing hubgy at night

On the 3/4. it was the second day I went to school and it was also so the first experience that I played rubgy at night !It was really unforgettable!

Around 7p.m., My Dad brought me to play rubgy in Kowloon Bay sports grand. When we were riding the minibus, it hadn't rained. Unluckily, when we dropped off at the station, it started to rain heavily! Therefore we ran quickly to the sports grounds.

Dad

When we arrived there, my dad introduced his teammates to me and shaked hands each other. Later on, the rain stopped a while so we went out to play rubgy. They were so kind and friendly! Surprisingly, my told me that they were the represent of Hong Kong team! At this moment, I was shocked and couldn't believe it! While we were playing the match, it started to rain again! However, we didn't stop and keep playing. It really very unforgettable moment because it shows how hard-working we Dave!

We played for 1 hour. Althought it's fun, my feet was started to pain again so my dad brought me back home. I hope I can play with them again in somedays!
