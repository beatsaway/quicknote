I think gene editing shouldn't be legal for professional athletes even though some people might like the point of gene editing. I will give some viewings for this topic.

For a heading, some people think it is good because it can lower the chances of serious or even fatal injuries for athletes. They think they can also make the time standing on a stadium longer than usual people who retire at their twenties.

In other cases, other people will reject it, like me. At first, only rich people can afford for it. That means their chance of winning is higher than other people. Also, in some worldwide competitions, like the Olympics, winners may be rewarded with money. I think it is almost no use if they have a lot of money.

Secondly, if the Cas-9 the scientists use accidentally cut the wrong DNA, it would make him or her lose something important and make them couldn't go onto the sports field anymore. It might be doomed for the athlete in the whole lifetime.

In a whole, I suppose it will be better if we don't use gene editing. Just do your best and be careful not to take it too serious.
