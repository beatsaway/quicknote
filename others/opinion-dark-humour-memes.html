About this piece

Opinion — dark humour memes

Write a short opinion piece (about 150–250 words) for a school forum or student magazine. State your view on whether people should send dark humour memes to others. Explain who may be hurt, acknowledge why some people still share them, and suggest a better alternative. Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits; the rest is the student’s draft as written.

  • Tone should be clear and thoughtful, not aggressive.
  • Use respectful wording when writing about disability (people with disabilities).

Draft length (student), about 115 words (room to add one example and a stronger ending).

Useful words (English · 粵語) dark humour(黑色幽默), meme(迷因／梗圖), offensive(冒犯), insensitive(欠敏感度), stressed(感到壓力), pessimistic(悲觀), empathy(同理心), counter-argument(反方論點), alternative(替代方法)

What went well

You take a clear stand and show you can see both sides.

  • 👍 Position. You argue that dark humour memes should not be sent, with reasons.
  • 👍 Impact. You think about disabled people, parents, and teachers, not only the sender.
  • 👍 Balance. Paragraph 2 explains why people still share them (busy life, stress, wanting fun).
  • 👍 Closing. You suggest finding another way to feel happy instead of relying on dark jokes.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Length. Add one concrete example of a meme type and one alternative (e.g. wholesome memes, chatting with friends).
  • 💡 Word choice. People feel stressed, not stressful; look forward to society → face society positively / trust society again.
  • 💡 Grammar. Watch they might make them (who is they?) and for making resting times → to make their break times.
  • 💡 Register. I guess they should sounds unsure for an opinion piece; try They should or In my view, they should.

I think people shouldn’t send dark humour memes to others, like if disabled people see other people laughing at people who might have disabilities, they might make them feel very stressful and pessimistic and not willing to look forward to society. Other people like the sender's parents and teachers will also feel annoyed.

However, a lot of people still send dark humour memes to others for fun nowadays. The reason might be the world now is busy and it makes students or workers feel unhappy. They use it for making resting times more interesting and funny. I guess they should use another idea for making them happy then.
