Article — keeping patient records private

Hospital records contain private health information. When medical data is not kept secure, serious harm can follow.

Write a short article (100–130 words). Give at least two examples of what could go wrong when medical data is leaked. Give two ideas for how hospitals or the government could protect medical data better.

  • Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits; the rest is the student’s draft as written.
  • Success criteria. At least two examples of harm. Two ideas for hospitals and/or government.

Draft length (student), about 170 words. Trim to 100–130 words by cutting repetition and rephrasing the long hospital sentence.

A lot of people now use e-hospital records to make seeing a doctor easier and faster. However, the medical data inside might leak out when you see a doctor.

At first, some people with rare diseases might don't want anyone else to know, as it might cause serious depression to the person. Even if that person only has illnesses like flu, they might not want to share them either.

Next, there are other personal information in it, like your name or your identity card number, so some people will take them and hack your computer. At that time, if you don't quickly change your passwords, your money will be taken away. Nobody can take your money back anymore.

Hospitals can create some anti-scamming systems to prevent them from hacking into patients' accounts and to fire and arrest the doctor(like in another article that I have seen). The government can also call the citizens to be aware while seeing a doctor. If they did anything that looked suspicious to you, you should ask them immediately.
