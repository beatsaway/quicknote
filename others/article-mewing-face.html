Is mewing going to help with your face?

You see a lot of videos online and people talking about how “mewing” can change your face to make you look more sigma. Before we can get into whether or not mewing is good for kids we have to learn about what mewing is and how it works.

What Is Mewing？

As for what mewing is, basically you close your lips and push your teeth together and then press your tongue to the roof of your mouth. Lots of people have been led to believe that if you hold your tongue to the roof of your mouth every day, then your face will start to change and your jaw will get stronger.

Is Mewing Good Practice for Kids?

There's No reason for Kids to Spend Time Practicing Mewing for a better face shape. All healthy children and teens need to do with their mouths closed is nose breathing, and that is all that is recommended. Keep in mind that forcing your tongue up against your bone can be very painful and even cause harm to teeth and gums.

Here are three reasons why kids should not practice mewing:

1. No scientific proof. Orthodontists and dentists are saying that there is no scientific proof to the claim made by tongue exercising that their practice can change the bone structure of the lower jaw. This is also what I have read, that there is no proof to back up these claims.

2. Kids' faces are already growing naturally. As children and teens grow, their jaws develop and teeth shift. Forcing pressure in the mouth will not make natural growth appear sooner or better.

3. Dangers of excessive force-As you can see adding excessive force with your tongue to push your teeth together can lead to a variety of problems including pain in your jaw muscles and even headaches and in extreme cases push your teeth out of alignment.

Fact vs. Online Trend

I separated the hype from the fact you can read in this writing.

The Fact: Resting your tongue gently on the roof of your mouth (with lips closed) and breathing through your nose is normal. The natural position of the tongue of mouth breathing over years can affect dental health and sleep quality.

The Online Trend Most importantly, the hype around “Mewing” as a way to beautify your face to change the shape of your jaw or become more attractive by using it as an alternative to orthodontics (braces) to straighten teeth is simply an internet trend and myth that needs to be debunked!

If you have trouble breathing through your nose, if your jaw clicks or even aches, or if you are worried about how your teeth line up then forget about the videos and talk to a dentist or orthodontist. They can check on the growth of your jaw and give you some real and effective advice on how to sort out your teeth!
