School magazine article — Switzerland in WW2

Task. Switzerland stayed officially neutral in World War II. A short video argues it still traded with both sides, accepted Nazi gold (some stolen) and turned away many Jewish refugees. Some people say a small country had little choice; others say neutrality does not mean moral innocence.

Write an article for your school magazine. Do you agree or disagree that Switzerland was truly neutral in World War II? Give three pieces of evidence.

  • Success criteria: clear agree or disagree; three pieces of evidence; a conclusion.
  • Only short yellow phrases are suggested edits; the rest is the draft as written.
  • Click flow. Yellow → green + comment. Only one comment at a time. Click the comment box, elsewhere, or green again to hide it. Click green with comment open to go back to yellow.

What went well

Clear disagree: peaceful on paper, not truly neutral.

  • 📌 Stance. You separate “peaceful” from “truly neutral” in line one.
  • 🥇 Gold + trade. Nazi gold and dealing with both sides match the video stimulus.
  • 🚪 Refugees. Turning Jewish people away is strong evidence on morality.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Last but not least. Swap Lastly for the success-criteria linker.

  • 💡 Past tense. Keep WW2 in the past: took, couldn’t, came.
  • 💡 Word bank. Use refugee, stolen, profit, morality where they fit.
  • 💡 Conclusion label. Start the last paragraph with In conclusion or To summarise.

Was Switzerland truly neutral in WW2?

I think Switzerland was peaceful, but not truly neutral in WW2. I will tell you some evidence to prove it.

First of all, it helped both sides of the war like taking Nazi's looted gold and trading with countries like the UK and America at the same time. This made them very rich when others can't even have a penny.

Secondly, they excluded anything that didn’t make income for them, like when the Jewish came for help, Switzerland turned them away for this reason, making them peaceful from doing almost nothing from foreigners.

Lastly, they take everything they can have, but didn’t let any of the people out or in. They didn’t kill any or help any. Their only mission is to take money, not to help the country to take more money and gold.

There are still who think Switzerland is a good country, though they don’t have mercy for anything. We should change our minds from thinking a WW2-style Switzerland is kind then
