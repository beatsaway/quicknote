Personal recount. The preparation of sports day

Topic. The preparation of sports day

Write about how you trained for sports day. Include what you did, how you felt, and what you hope for.

  • Success criteria. Clear goal. Training details. Feelings. A short hope or conclusion.
  • Only short yellow phrases are suggested edits. The rest is the draft as written.
  • Click flow. Yellow to green plus comment. Only one comment at a time.
Useful words sports day, MVP, C grade, long jump, sprint, every Sunday, exhausted, exhausting, Sports Ground, Although, in vain, I hope

What went well

Clear goal plus two Sunday training days with real places.

  • 🏅 Clear target. MVP for C grade girls is a strong wish.
  • 🏃 Places. King’s Park, Choi Wing Road, Hammer Hill make the training feel real.
  • 💪 Feelings. Exhausted / drained shows how hard you worked.

Could be even better

  • 💡 wish / hope. I hope I can or I wish I could.
  • 💡 exhausted vs exhausting. You feel exhausted. Training is exhausting.
  • 💡 Sunday. every Sunday (not every Sundays). on Sunday.
  • 💡 Although. Do not use Although … but. Add be in won’t be in vain.

The preparation of sports day

We will hold a sports day on the 13th-14th of October. This year, I wish I can get the MVP of Сgrade girls record so I train running and long Jump with my friends on every Sundays.

The first Sunday, we went to King's Park to practise short run in the morning. Then we went to Choi Wing Road Sports ground to practise long run. I' think Sunday is most exhausting because in the weekdays you still sit in classroom to listen and write. However in Sunday, you need move so I feel very exhausting!

The second Sunday, we went to Hammer Hill Sports ground to train Long jump. This Sunday made me feel more drained because I need to use a lot of power to jump.

Although it's so exhausting in these two Sundays but I think my work won't in vain. I hope I can be the MVP in C grade record!

Comments

Lively sports-day training recount with clear Sundays and a strong MVP wish. Fix hope/wish, exhausted, and Although … but.

ENClear goal. You want the MVP for the C grade girls’ record. Readers know why you train.

粵目標清楚。 你想攞 C 級女仔紀錄 MVP。讀者知你點解練。

ENTwo Sundays. King’s Park, Choi Wing Road, and Hammer Hill make the preparation concrete.

粵兩個星期日。 京士柏、彩榮路、斧山，令準備過程好具體。

ENwish / hope. Use I hope I can or I wish I could. Also every Sunday and long jump.

粵wish／hope。 用 I hope I can 或 I wish I could。另 every Sunday 同 long jump。

ENexhausted. You feel exhausted. The training is exhausting. Also need to move and on Sunday.

粵exhausted。 你覺得 exhausted。訓練係 exhausting。另 need to move 同 on Sunday。

ENEnding. Do not write Although … but. Change to Although …, I think … and won’t be in vain.

粵結尾。 唔好 Although … but。改 Although …, I think … 同 won’t be in vain。
