Article — Antarctica and global warming

Antarctica is changing as the planet gets warmer. The effects are not only about the ice.

Write a short article (100–130 words). Mention four things that could happen to Antarctica because of global warming.

  • Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits; the rest is the student’s draft as written.
  • Success criteria. One political effect. One ecological effect. One economic effect. One scientific effect.

Draft length (student), about 215 words.

Useful words (from the task) (English · 粵語) global warming(全球暖化), ice sheet(冰蓋), sea level(海平面), ecosystem(生態系統), treaty(條約), research funding(研究資金), tourism(旅遊業), habitat(棲息地), vulnerable(脆弱), climate data(氣候數據)

As the Earth gets hotter Antarctica is going to change in ways that will affect a lot of other places too.

Political Effect: When the ice in Antarctica melts it will be easier for ships to travel through and we will find resources but this will also cause problems between countries that want to control the land and make decisions about it under the Antarctic Treaty.

Ecological Effect: The ice in the ocean around Antarctica is getting smaller and this is hurting the homes of animals called krill, which is a big problem for all the other animals that live in the ocean like penguins and whales.

Economic Effect: Antarctica melting is causing the sea to rise. This is a threat to cities and towns near the ocean all around the world but at the same time more people will want to visit Antarctica because the weather will be warmer and they can travel there for a longer time.

Scientific Effect: The big pieces of ice in Antarctica are melting fast and this is destroying old ice that had important information about the climate, which is bad for scientists who want to learn about the past.

All these changes show that Antarctica is in trouble and it is very important, for the future of our planet, Antarctica. Antarctica plays a role in the future of the Earth.
