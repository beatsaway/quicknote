Pros and cons when you have siblings

Nowadays, there are a lot of parents just born one baby but also some parents would born more than one baby. So it's good to have siblings or don't have siblings? Let’s find out together!

First of all, having siblings can be a good things because they will play with you so you won’t feel bored easily. And also they may help you when you are in trouble or in need. As example of me, one time I got a bad exam result and my sister Anna comforted me immediately which made me felt relaxed a bit.

However sometimes it can quite annoying too when your siblings are always disturbing you, especially the siblings who are younger than you. They will sometimes non-stop to disturb you and make you feel disturbed or even angry. Like when I was preparing for the exams, my sister and brother will bother me to play with them. Although they (younger) are trouble-making but still after I finished studying, I will play with them.

Actually, there are more examples of “Pros” and “Cons” when you have siblings. However what we should do is that cherish every moments with them even they are trouble-making because not everyone will have siblings!
