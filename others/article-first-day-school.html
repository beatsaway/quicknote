School writing — the first day in school

Topic. The first day in school

Write about your first day back. Include what happened in the morning, recess, lunch and how you felt.

  • Success criteria: clear time order; feelings; past tense for finished events.
  • Only short yellow phrases are suggested edits; the rest is the draft as written.
  • Click flow. Yellow → green + comment. Only one comment at a time.
Useful words first day of school, class teacher, strict / discipline, eyebrows, relieved, went back, recognised, interesting, homework, every day, First of all, Then, During, Finally

What went well

Clear day timeline: morning → recess → lunch → no homework.

  • 💇 Real detail. Hair / eyebrows + clip makes the class-teacher moment vivid.
  • 😊 Feelings. Excited → shocked → relieved — good emotional arc.
  • 👥 Friends. Form 1 visit + Form 2 lunch with friends fits the first-day topic.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Past tense. talk → talked; keep one main tense for the day.
  • 💡 Word form. eyebrowns, interested → interesting, recognised me.
  • 💡 Missing verbs. I back to → I went back to.
  • 💡 Agreement. no homework (is); every day was the same.

The first day in school

XX on the 1st of September , it is also the first day in school. I was so excited to meet my friends again so I woke up early. Also I am very excited to meet my new 2 class teacher!

XX when I arrived at school , I saw my friends and 2 new teacher. Unfortunately my class teacher is a disciplined teacher. I was so surprised and shocked about that because she always says my hair is over the eyebrowns and she told me to cut it but I didn't. I hope she won't catch me this time. Surprisingly, she didn't catch me and she just said that to clip my hair. At that moment I felt relieved a lot and it is the first time I think she is a good disciplined teacher!

XX During the recess , I back to my form one class to meet new students. Some of them were recognized me so we chatted for a while about the new school life.

XX During lunch time I went out with my friends to eat at lunch. It is the first day of having lunch with my friends in Form 2. We talk a lot about how about what we are doing during summer holiday and I think they're sharing is quite interested too!

What I like is that there are no homework for us to do in the first of school! I wish everyday are same in the future!

Comments

Lively first-day story with clear scenes. Fix past tense, spelling, and a few broken phrases.

ENTimeline. Morning, teacher, recess, lunch, homework — easy to follow.

ENPast tense. was / talked / did / liked for that finished day.

粵過去式。 講嗰日用 was／talked／did／liked。

ENWord form. eyebrows, went back, recognised me, interesting, their.

粵詞形。 eyebrows、went back、recognised me、interesting、their。
