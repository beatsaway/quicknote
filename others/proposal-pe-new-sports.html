Proposal — three new sports for PE

Your PE teacher wants fresh ideas for next term. Normal football and basketball are getting boring. You have been learning about weird sports from around the world.

Write a short proposal to your PE teacher. Suggest three new kinds of sports to try in PE lessons. For each sport, mention one main rule and one reason it would be fun or useful for students.

  • Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits; the rest is the student’s draft as written.
  • Success criteria. Three new sports suggested. One main rule for each sport. One reason each sport would be fun or useful.

Draft length (student), about 165 words.

Useful words (from the task) (English · 粵語) proposal(建議書), PE lesson(體育課), suggest(建議), rule(規則), teamwork(團隊合作), balance(平衡), obstacle(障礙), relay(接力), safe(安全), equipment(器材), inclusive(包容), fun(有趣), skill(技巧), strategy(策略), sports day(運動會)

What went well

You have a clear sport in mind and some good details.

  • 👍 Pickleball idea. You explain that it mixes tennis and badminton.
  • 👍 Easy to start. You mention light rackets, light balls and simple rules.
  • 👍 Personal voice. You say what you like about the sport and why you want to play it.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Three sports. The task asks for three sports. You mainly write about pickleball.
  • 💡 Rule + reason each. Label one clear rule and one PE reason for every sport.
  • 💡 Proposal tone. Write to your PE teacher. Focus on fun, skills and safety in school, not the government.
  • 💡 Task vocabulary. Work in proposal, suggest, teamwork, inclusive or equipment where they fit.

4/7 New Sports – Pickleball

Nowadays, many people invent a wide range of new sports. For example, rugby, AR dodgeball, run with trackmill in the water and pickleball. Today, I would like to introduce a new sport game which called, Pickleball.

Pickleball is a new sport game which combine with tennis and badminton. It’s easy to play and understand. It’s suitable for elderly and children to play because the ball and the racket are light and the movement are less. Therefore, pickleball not just a kind of new sport but also can help elderly to stay healthy too!

What I like pickleball the most is that it's cheap and to learn the game rules. However I think there are not enough pickleball playground for people to play in HongKong which I think the government should be improve.

I hope there are more people to know pickleball and become hit in someday so I can play and meet friends with them more.

Comments

You already explain pickleball well. The yellow bits help you match the proposal task and add two more sports with a rule and a reason each.

ENOther ideas in your intro. You already name rugby, AR dodgeball and pickleball. Two of those could become your second and third sports.

粵開首有其他 idea。 你已提 rugby、AR dodgeball、pickleball，其中兩個可以作第二、第三個運動。

ENThree sports. Give three short sections, e.g. Pickleball, AR dodgeball, obstacle relay. One main rule and one fun or useful reason for each.

ENState the rule. For pickleball, say one rule clearly, e.g. serves must be underhand, or the ball must bounce once on each side before you volley.

粵寫清楚規則。 匹克球可寫一條規則，例如發球要下手，或者每邊要先彈地一次先可以抽击。
