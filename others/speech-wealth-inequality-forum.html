Speech — wealth inequality and children’s education

School–community forum: parents, teachers, and students. Explain why wealth inequality is a problem and give practical ways to support children from low-income or financially struggling families with education (target about 250–300 words).

  • Audience: mixed backgrounds; the speech should not sound as if you blame pupils for poverty.
  • Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits; the rest is the student’s draft as written.

Draft length (student): about 130 words · well under the 250–300 target—room to add a greeting, examples, and a short ending.

Useful words (from the task) (English · 粵語) wealth inequality(財富不均), low-income families(低收入家庭), financial hardship(經濟困難), opportunity gap(機會差距), frugal(節儉), subsidised(有津貼／資助), social mobility(社會流動), vulnerable families(弱勢家庭), level the playing field(拉平起點), forum(社區論壇), audience(聽眾), enrichment(課外增潤), early intervention(及早支援), meritocracy(唯才是用)

What went well

You name the big idea — wealth inequality — and you link it to children and school in a simple, clear way.

  • 🎯 Focus — child labour, rent stress, and the contrast with the very rich are all on topic.
  • 🤲 Solutions — you mention donations and gifts in kind (clothes, books, toys), which fits “practical support.”
  • 🗣️ Voice — phrases like lifeless show you are trying to make the problem feel real.

Could be even better

  • 💡 Length & shape: add a greeting to the audience, 1–2 concrete examples (e.g. school meals, bursary), and a short thank-you / call to act to reach 250–300 words.
  • 💡 Word choice: Oppositely is not natural for contrast; On the other hand / By contrast is standard.
  • 💡 Grammar & tone: under living in is not idiomatic—use from low-income families. The line you should work harder can sound blaming in a forum; try we can support them so that they can…
  • 💡 Task terms: try weaving in opportunity gap, subsidised, or level the playing field in one or two sentences.

Speech (draft script)

In the world nowadays, wealth inequality is a very serious problem. It makes lots of children need to work early and they couldn’t go to school. Wealth inequality can sometimes make them homeless as they can’t pay their house rent. Oppositely, millionaires could buy anything they want, maybe with people serving them. The difference is becoming bigger. I think if it keeps on like this, countries will become weaker and it will become "lifeless" eventually.

We can donate our money out or take part in other donating activities, such as donating clothes, books, toys or others. For the children under living in low-income families, you should work harder and try to make more money in the future.

Comments

The argument is on topic and easy to follow. Tighten contrast words (By contrast / The gap is widening) and fix the last sentence so the community sounds helpful, not critical of the students.
