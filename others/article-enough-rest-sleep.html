School newspaper — sleepy students and enough rest

You are a reporter for the school newspaper. You have noticed that many students in your school are always sleepy in lessons.

Write an article for the school newspaper. Give two reasons why it is important to get enough rest at night. Give two suggestions for your classmates to improve their sleep habits.

  • Task pictures. Sleepy students in class. Coffee before bed. Phones and screens at night.
  • Only the yellow word groups are suggested edits; the rest is the student’s draft as written.
  • Success criteria. Two reasons for enough rest. Two suggestions for better sleep habits.

Draft length (student), about 245 words.

The importance of having enough rest

In these few days, many students in the school are always sleepy in lessons. How can we improve this situation? Let’s explore together.

First of all, getting enough rest can help you stay more focus in the next day. You will pay more attention during the lessons. Therefore you won’t find it difficult to complete the exams, tests and quizzes.

Besides, getting enough sleep can also maintain your health. Staying up late will be more easy to catch a cold and lower your immunity. Therefore it's important to not staying up late.

After we explore the importance of having enough rest. Let's find out how can we get rid of these problem.

One way to improve is to not drink coffee before you sleep. This is because there are some cocaine in coffee which makes you felt excited and can't sleep easily. To fall asleep easily, you can drink a cup of warm water or milk to help you. This way, you can improve your sleeping habits.

Another suggestions is to not using phone or eletrical devices before you sleep. It’s because phone will have blu-ray which you can't have a good sleep quality. To have a better sleeping quality, I suggest you can put away phone and turn it into "Don't disturb" mode before you sleep so it can help you get rid of it.

It’s important to have enough rest. Let’s get started today to help you reduce your sleepy to have better sleep quality and rest!
