Website post (about 150–200 words)

Three ways our shelter stands with rough sleepers

At night, homelessness is not a statistic: it is neighbours without a safe bed or a clear next step. At our shelter, we want every rough sleeper who comes in to be met with respect, not pity.

Unsafe nights and exhausted health

Sleeping in doorways or on a void deck means cold, noise, and strain. We offer a transitional shelter: clean beds, washing facilities, and meals served with dignity. Caseworkers draw up confidential plans so each guest can see the next step.

Isolation and emotional distress

Many guests carry trauma or anxiety in silence. Counseling, listening groups, and a consistent welcome from staff remind people they matter and are not alone.

No income, no pathway to stable rent

Without work, saving for long-term housing can feel impossible. Skills sessions, job introductions, and help with documents build a realistic pathway. Outreach teams invite people still on the street inside. Donor gifts fund travel, ID fees, and learning materials for adults and children.

One shelter cannot fix every gap in the system—but together we keep the door open and hope within sight. Thank you for standing with us.
